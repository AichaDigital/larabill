# AID-1997 — One-off sale without vouchers (larabill 9.6.0)

- **Status:** draft, revision 3 — gate rounds 1-2 adjudicated (§11); D8 (refund) must be redesigned as revision 4 before the TDD; no code written.
- **Tickets:** AID-1997 (umbrella), AID-2010 (C1), AID-2011 (C2), AID-2012 (C3 + C4).
- **Base:** v9.5.0 (`c04671f`).
- **Release type:** minor (9.6.0). Additive API, one new table, one new nullable column; no existing behaviour changes for rows the sale never touches.
- **Consumer:** ArabeAicha (courses, one-off card payment; Stripe is only the gateway). Conformance: ADE-1260.

## 1. Problem

A consumer sells a one-off item. The sale must be frozen before the charge, the confirmed charge must be checked against what was frozen BEFORE a fiscal number is consumed, and an invoicing failure after a confirmed charge must resume without charging twice or numbering twice.

In 9.5.0 those guarantees exist only inside `VoucherSaleService`, bound to a voucher/credit model this sale does not have:

- `convertAndIssue()` leaves the invoice with collection status `DRAFT`; `Ledger::payableStatuses()` excludes `DRAFT` (`Ledger.php:219`), so `receive()` cannot allocate to it. The only writer of `DRAFT → PENDING` is `VoucherSaleService::back()`.
- A proforma stays fiscally editable until conversion, and here conversion happens after the charge. The re-freeze guard (`Invoice::assertNotUnfrozenAfterConversion()`, `Invoice.php:253`) only protects a proforma claimed by a voucher sale.
- The frozen expectation (amount, currency, payer) bound to a key exists only in `VoucherSaleService::claim()`, which also requires the payment at claim time (`grouped_payment_id` NOT NULL, migration 055) — there is no pre-charge stage.
- An issuer currency change between freeze and issuance is not seen by `FiscalChangeDetector`; `issue()` regenerates the snapshots from the live config (AID-1649), so it would be frozen into a numbered document. Only the voucher claim refuses it before the number (`InvoiceService.php:420-428`).

Product decision (closed, not reopened here): no voucher, no credit balance, no `sellVoucher`/`consumeVoucher`, not even provisionally. **The one-off sale MUST NOT cause any `credit_movements` row, at any stage and after it** (see D6).

## 2. Decisions

### D1 — Separate reservation table; voucher machinery extended only at its entry points

A new `sale_reservations` table and `invoices.sale_reservation_id`, NOT a generalisation of `voucher_sale_claims`.

- **Rejected alternative:** reuse `voucher_sale_claims` with a `kind` column. It would inherit the guard points, but `grouped_payment_id` is NOT NULL (the claim is born with its payment), so the pre-charge stage needs an ALTER of the payment-at-creation contract; it would also inherit the permanent voucher refusals keyed on `sale_claim_id` (`Ledger.php:191`, `SettlementService.php:535`) that are wrong for a one-off sale. Gate round 1 confirmed the reuse is not safer.
- **What changes in the voucher code:** only refusals at its two entry points that do not know about reservations (`VoucherSaleService::claim()` and `cancelProforma()`, §5 G14-G15). Voucher behaviour for proformas without a reservation is unchanged.
- **Cost:** every guard point that consults the voucher claim must consult the reservation too (inventory in §5). A missed point is a hole; the inventory is a gate item and a test item (§7, «census»).

### D2 — Stages

| Stage | Value | Meaning | Proforma | Payment | Invoice |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Reserved` | 0 | frozen before the charge | `is_immutable`, DRAFT/SENT | none | none |
| `Paid` | 1 | confirmed charge recorded, no number yet | frozen | POSTED, no allocation, no entry | none, or PREPARED |
| `Issuing` | 2 | `issue()` may consume the number | frozen / CONVERTED | POSTED | PREPARED or ISSUED |
| `Settled` | 3 | invoice ISSUED, one allocation of the full payment, PAID at settlement | CONVERTED | POSTED, fully allocated | ISSUED |
| `Released` | 4 | abandoned before any charge | unfrozen | none | none |

`Settled` and `Released` are terminal. Settlement writes `settled_at` and the ids of the settlement allocation and payment on the reservation; those are the immutable **evidence of settlement** (D7).

### D3 — The expectation is read from the proforma, not passed in

`reserve()` freezes `expected_amount = proforma.total_amount`, `expected_currency = proforma's fiscal config currency`, `expected_billable_user_id = proforma.billable_user_id`, under the proforma row lock. The consumer cannot declare an expectation that differs from the document.

### D4 — A mismatching charge is refused with zero writes

If the confirmed charge does not match the expectation (amount, currency, payer), `settle()` throws a typed exception and writes nothing: no payment row, no stage change. The money exists at the gateway; refunding it there is the consumer's job (out of scope). Recording a non-matching payment would leave an unallocatable POSTED payment that no package path resolves.

### D5 — C1 is a named public transition

`InvoiceService::openForCollection(Invoice): Invoice` — ISSUED document + collection `DRAFT` → `PENDING`, under the invoice lock, checked write. Idempotent: an ISSUED invoice already past `DRAFT` is returned unchanged. Refused (typed exception) on a non-ISSUED document, a proforma, and an invoice carrying a non-terminal reservation or an open voucher claim (their own settlement is the only writer). `settle()` S4 uses the same transition internally (one code path).

### D6 — Provenance protection is permanent once a charge was recorded

A reservation that ever reached `Paid` protects its rows forever, not only until `Settled` (gate round 1, findings 1-2):

- The proforma can never be reopened: no unfreeze, no write of `status`/`converted_invoice_id`/`converted_at` back to an open state, no deletion — the voucher's «ever claimed» rule (`Invoice.php:263`) applied to reservations.
- `invoices.sale_reservation_id` is never cleared or changed once written.
- The settlement allocation is never reversed through `PaymentService::reverseAllocation()`: for a direct allocation that path mints a `SURPLUS` entry (`PaymentService.php:531-540`), which D1's product rule forbids.
- Money goes back only through `OneOffSaleService::refund()` (D8), which creates no credit. The plain document operations (`SettlementService::rectify()`, `annul()`) stay available on the settled invoice, because neither writes credit.

Only a reservation `Released` at `Reserved` (never charged) leaves no protection behind.

### D7 — Replay of a settled sale proves the evidence, not today's state

A `Settled` replay returns the result built from the settlement evidence (reservation ids, `settled_at`, the allocation row by id). It does not require the invoice to be PAID now, and it never re-runs the original empty-allocation `Full` payment request (its allocation map no longer matches once S4 wrote the allocation, `PaymentService.php:278`). `diagnose()` reports a later lawful document operation (rectification, annulment) as such, not as corruption.

### D8 — Refund: a mechanism, not a policy (owner decision, 2026-11-14; revision 4, 2026-11-15)

A settled sale can be refunded, in full or in part, once or several times up to the settled amount, at any moment and for any reason the consumer decides. The package checks no withdrawal window, no legal cause and no business rule: a legal withdrawal (desistimiento) is one use case among many, and the commercial and accounting practice of the consumer is its own (ADR-014: larabill issues fiscal documents and records money, it has no commercial policy). An optional free-text `reason` is stored for the consumer's audit and never interpreted.

Revision 4 redesigns the refund around the rectifying invoice with NO credit balance, per the round-2 adjudication (§11). Owner guidance on the redesign: accounting and invoicing are more creative and usable than legalist perfectionism — the mechanism must be fiscally correct and usable, not a legal treatise. Four properties, each closing a measured defect:

- **A durable intent first (R2-3).** The refund's first write is the `sale_refunds` intent row — the full payload (sale, resolved amount, method, reference, `refundedAt`, `recorded_by`, `reason`) frozen under the reservation lock, BEFORE any irreversible issuance. The intent RESERVES capacity: remaining refundable is computed and consumed under the same lock, so two concurrent refunds with different keys cannot exceed the settled amount. A cut after the intent leaves a row a re-call by key resumes; it never leaks capacity to another key.
- **Lines from the gross amount, conserved across refunds (R2-2; R3-1, R3-2, V1, V3, R5-1, R5-2, R5-S3).** The consumer asks to give back a GROSS amount; the package never reads it as a taxable base (`differenceItems()` is not used). Two paths, chosen at R1:

  - **Pristine full refund** (no prior non-`Abandoned` refunds and the resolved gross equals the original's total gross): the plan is the original's frozen lines VERBATIM, sign-flipped — one plan entry per ORIGINAL LINE with its complete fiscal components (base, tax, gross, tax entries). This reverses multi-tax lines exactly (a pair-bucket split would duplicate the shared base — round 5 probe: `[-113,-18,-131]` instead of the correct `[-100,-31,-131]`), zero-gross rounding artifacts (a line whose gross is 0 while base/tax are not — round 5 probe: an original of `[101, -1, 100]` must fully reverse as base −101, tax +1) and discount lines, each with its own sign.
  - **Every other refund** (partial, or full after partials): the plan is a COMPLETE component allocation per rate bucket (bucket identity, allocated base, allocated tax, allocated gross, tax entries), computed at R1 under the reservation lock from the frozen bucket components minus the complete stored plans of prior non-`Abandoned` refunds; gross pro-rata with largest-remainder correction so Σ allocated gross = the resolved gross exactly; base = `round_half_up(remBase · gross_b / remGross)`; tax = gross_b − base; an exhausted bucket takes its frozen components verbatim; a bucket whose ALLOCATED gross is 0 emits no line, its components staying in the remaining amounts. **The terminal refund sweeps (R6-1):** when the resolved gross equals the whole remaining refundable, the plan must bring EVERY bucket's remaining components to exactly 0 — including a bucket whose remaining gross is 0 while its remaining base/tax are not (ordinary per-line rounding creates such buckets, `TaxCalculationService.php:93`): its outstanding base/tax are allocated verbatim as a plan entry with gross 0, and the refund path emits that zero-gross line (its identity holds: base + tax = 0). Without the sweep, sequential refunds leave the residue un-reversed forever (round 6 probe: original `[101, -1, 100]`, two 50-refunds → remaining `[1, -1, 0]` never reversed). Multi-tax lines refuse this path loud (AID-589) — only a pristine full refund reverses them.
- The plan is stored on the intent (`bucket_plan`, §6) and NEVER recomputed; R2 builds the lines from the stored plan verbatim. The refund path validates its OWN lines (per-line identity, frozen rate pairs, per-line sign matching its plan entry's sign, cross-entry Σ `total = −gross`); the ordinary public `rectify(items:)` keeps the G10 strictly-negative contract untouched (AID-1778 unchanged).
- **Refund-owned issuance (R2-4, R2-5; R3-3, R3-4, R3-6).** The rectificative is created by the refund route through the two-phase creation contract with the intent's link IN THE CREATION DATA: the durable unnumbered DRAFT carries `settlement_operation_key = refundKey` AND `sale_refund_id = intent.id` atomically from phase 1, and the intent's `rectificative_invoice_id` is written in the same phase-1 transaction — ownership exists at creation, not after issuance. The refund route passes NO post-issuance collection stamp: the rectificative is born collection-DRAFT; neither the creation stamp (`InvoiceService.php:286`) nor the adoption stamp (`SettlementService.php:359-361`) — both unchecked `save()` — is reused. Adoption or resume by key verifies the invoice-side intent link before any `issue()`; a document carrying the key without the link is a typed conflict with zero writes, never issued.
- **A reader contract (R2-6, re-measured; PDF claim corrected in round 3).** The money truth of a sale is its refund-aware reader: `refunded()`, `netSettled()` and `remainingRefundable()` on the reservation (§4.5). The rectificative's document axis keeps `amountOutstanding()` negative by design (AID-962 DECISION 6, `Invoice.php:929-934`). The PDF builder already renders no collection section for an allocation-free rectificative (`DomPDFService:552` returns null, so the template condition at `fiscal.blade.php:239` is false — round 3's probe: `amountOutstanding=-12100, pdf_collection=null`); rev4's claim that the template would print a negative `amount_due` was measured wrong and is withdrawn. `CustomerCredit::outstanding()`/`balance()` read 0 once both documents are PAID (the open set excludes PAID, `CustomerCredit.php:119-130`).

No `credit_movements` row is written, no allocation is reversed, and the funding payment stays POSTED: the original invoice keeps its allocation and stays PAID; the rectificative documents the reduction; the refund row documents the money that left. Customer position after a full refund: credit balance 0, outstanding 0.

**External rectification (R2-S2, declared without policy):** a rectification issued outside `refund()` corrects the document axis only; it neither creates nor consumes refund capacity, which is money-based (settled amount − Σ refund rows). Reconciling an external correction with refunds already paid is the consumer's accounting decision; larabill records both axes and refuses nothing on policy grounds. `annul()` of the settled invoice keeps refusing (allocated invoice, existing guard); the refund path never relies on it.

**Currency (R2-7, R3-5; rev4's bypass claim was false and is withdrawn).** `createInvoice` phase 2 always runs `issue()` for a non-draft request (`InvoiceService.php:281`), and issuance regenerates all three snapshots from the ROW's referenced config at the issuance instant (`InvoiceService.php:472-488`) — a pre-issuance check of the ACTIVE config binds nothing (round 3's mutation proved it: removing the final guard while keeping the early check issued a USD document). The refund's binding currency check therefore lives INSIDE issuance, pre-number (the G7 pattern): the config instance that will price the snapshots must resolve the sale's frozen currency; a drift is a typed refusal before the correlative is consumed. R2a keeps a fast-fail pre-check of the same condition for cheap refusal.

**Inherited defect scoped into 9.6.0 (R3-8, measured).** `issue()` regenerates `customer_snapshot` from the referenced profile whenever `user_tax_profile_id` is set (`InvoiceService.php:488`), so today's `rectify()` breaks its own frozen-recipient promise — round 3 changed the profile after the original's emission and the rectificative carried the changed tax id. This blocks D8's fiscal correctness and is fixed within AID-1997: issuance preserves a supplied frozen recipient, and the refund's pre-number checks verify the emitted rectificative carries the original's recipient.

## 3. Public surface (`@api`, listed in `docs/api-surface.md`)

```php
final class OneOffSaleService
{
    public function reserve(SaleReservationRequest $request): SaleReservation;      // C2
    public function settle(SaleSettlementRequest $request): OneOffSaleResult;       // C3 + C4
    public function release(string $operationKey, string $actor): SaleReservation;  // C2, abandoned checkout
    public function diagnose(string $operationKey): SaleReservationState;           // C4
    public function refund(SaleRefundRequest $request): SaleRefundResult;           // D8
}

// InvoiceService
public function openForCollection(Invoice $invoice): Invoice;                       // C1
```

- **`SaleReservationRequest`:** `proformaId`, `operationKey` (≤191 chars).
- **`SaleSettlementRequest`:** `operationKey`, `billableUserId`, `amount` (FixedDecimal), `currency`, `receivedAt`, `method` (PaymentMethod), `reference`, `recordedBy`.
- **`OneOffSaleResult`:** `invoice`, `payment`, `allocation`, `reservation`.
- **`SaleReservationState`:** stage, proforma id, invoice id or null, payment id or null, allocation id or null, `issuedBySale`, and `?SaleIntegrityReason` when the persisted tuple contradicts the stage.
- **`SaleRefundRequest`:** `operationKey` (the sale's), `refundKey` (≤191 chars, the refund's own idempotency key), `amount` (FixedDecimal, the GROSS amount to give back; null = the whole remaining refundable, resolved and frozen at intent time, R2-S3), `refundedAt`, `method`, `reference`, `recordedBy`, `reason` (nullable, free text).
- **`SaleRefundResult`:** `refund` (`SaleRefund`, the intent row), `rectificative` (nullable — null only on the replay of an `Abandoned` intent, which owns no document, V4), `remainingRefundable`.
- **Refund reader contract (R2-6, V4):** `SaleReservation::refunded()` (Σ `Refunded`-stage rows), `netSettled()` (settled − refunded), `remainingRefundable()` (settled − Σ amounts of non-`Abandoned` refund rows — pending intents reserve capacity, abandoned ones release it; ONE predicate, used by the reader, by R1's capacity check and by recovery alike).
- **`SaleReservation`**, **`SaleRefund`** models: read-only for consumers; written only by the package.

All four service methods call `OutermostTransaction::assert()` as their first statement (AID-1884 contract). `settle()` commits its stages separately and `issue()` runs as its OWN outermost transaction, never inside a stage wrapper: nested, Laravel uses a savepoint and a deadlock escapes instead of being retried by `issue()` (vendor `ManagesTransactions.php:93`, `:164`). Raising `base_transaction_level` does not restore independent commits.

## 4. Behaviour

### 4.0 Lock protocol (one order for every path)

Global order, extending the existing one (payment → proforma → invoice): **payment → proforma → invoice → reservation**. The reservation is always locked LAST, the same place the model guards lock a claim (document row first, then its claim — `Invoice.php:215`, `:235`). Every path below follows it; a path that has no payment or no invoice yet simply skips that step. Lookup by `operation_key` is a plain (non-locking) read used only to find the ids; the decisions are taken on the rows re-read under the locks, in order. **Restart rule (R2-S1):** the lock set is re-read under the locks after taking them; if the required set grew between discovery and lock (a rectificative came into existence, a second invoice row appeared), the path releases and restarts the whole sequence in the global order — it never locks a member out of order.

### 4.1 `reserve()` (one transaction)

1. Plain read of any reservation with this key (to find its proforma).
2. Lock the proforma, then lock every non-`Released` reservation of the proforma and the key's reservation.
3. Key's reservation exists: on this proforma → return it (replay, expectation NOT recomputed); on another proforma → `IdempotencyConflictException::forKey`.
4. A non-`Released` reservation of this proforma exists with another key → `SaleReservedException`. (Lookup is «all live reservations of the proforma», never `.first()` over history.)
5. Proforma not a proforma, status ∉ {DRAFT, SENT}, CONVERTING/CONVERTED, claimed by a voucher sale, total not positive, or no fiscal config currency → typed refusal.
6. Write the reservation (stage `Reserved`, frozen expectation) and the freeze (`is_immutable = true`, `immutable_at`) under the reservation's authorization, both checked writes (`CheckedWrite::orFail`; a vetoing listener aborts the transaction). `makeImmutable()` is NOT used: it ignores the save result (`Invoice.php:483`).
7. Re-read the proforma and the reservation before commit and assert the persisted state.

### 4.2 `release()` (one transaction)

Lock proforma → reservation. Allowed at `Reserved` only. Unfreezes the proforma under the reservation's authorization, sets stage `Released` with `released_at`/`released_by`; checked writes; persisted state re-read. The key is spent. At `Paid` or later → `SaleStageException`. A released reservation does not block a new reservation of the same proforma with another key.

### 4.3 `settle()` — four stages, each resumable by the same key

- **S1 — record the charge (one transaction).** Locks: (existing payment of the reservation, if any) → proforma → reservation. Missing → `SaleReservationNotFoundException`; `Released` → refused.
  - At `Reserved`: compare payer, currency and amount with the frozen expectation AND with the locked proforma (total, payer, config currency — the drift check, D4); any mismatch → typed exception, zero writes. **Ownership before adoption:** a `GroupedPayment` already carrying `idempotency_key = operationKey` is refused with `IdempotencyConflictException` (it is not this sale's: no reservation names it). Then record the payment through `PaymentService::process()` with `ReceiveRequest::forOneOffSale()` — no allocations, no remainder entry — under `ReplayPolicy::Full`; a lost race on the key UNIQUE is a conflict, never an adoption. Assert after the write: the payment has no allocation and no credit movement. Stage → `Paid`, `grouped_payment_id` written (UNIQUE). Checked writes.
  - At `Paid` or later: the payment named by the reservation must match the request (payer, currency, amount, method, reference, `receivedAt` to the second in UTC); otherwise `IdempotencyConflictException::forKey`. No call to `process()`.
- **S2 — convert (one transaction).** Locks: payment → proforma → (PREPARED invoice if any) → reservation. Re-check the payment against the reservation (POSTED, not reversed, values unchanged). Under the reservation's authorization, convert; the PREPARED invoice gets `sale_reservation_id` in the same transaction. An existing conversion is adopted only if it carries this reservation's link.
- **S3 — issue.** (a) One transaction, locks payment → proforma → invoice → reservation: revalidate payment, invoice (total, payer, link, no allocation, collection DRAFT, no `paid_at`) and the locked config currency; stage → `Issuing`. (b) Then `issue()` as its own outermost transaction under the reservation's authorization. Inside `issue()`, before the number, under the locks it already takes plus the reservation: the same three checks (G7). `issued_by_sale` is written in the issuance transaction.
- **S4 — collect (one transaction).** Locks: payment → proforma → invoice → reservation. Revalidate; ISSUED and `issued_by_sale` required; `DRAFT → PENDING` through the C1 transition; ONE allocation of the full payment (`Ledger::createAllocation()`); `recomputeCollectionState()` → PAID; stage → `Settled` with `settled_at` and `allocation_id`; checked writes; re-read the whole tuple (one allocation, PAID, zero credit movements for the payment) before commit.

A `Settled` reservation is a replay (D7). An incoherent tuple throws `SaleIntegrityException` with zero writes.

### 4.4 `diagnose()`

Read-only, inside ONE transaction that takes shared locks in the global order (payment → proforma → invoice → reservation, `sharedLock()`), so it never combines rows from different commits of S3/S4. Returns the stage, the ids and the first `SaleIntegrityReason` whose predicate fails for that stage (the same predicates `settle()` applies); for `Settled` it checks the evidence (D7).

### 4.5 `refund()` — three steps over a durable intent, resumable by the refund key

**The verification primitive (rev11, owner decision A; closes R9-2, R9-4, R9-5, R9-S2).** Every verification read of the refund — R1's plan source, phase 1, pre-number, post-save, R3's post-close check and post-commit detection — goes through ONE package primitive: a bare PDO SELECT on the document's exact WRITE connection (`Connection::getPdo()` — the write PDO by construction), returning raw persisted columns. No Eloquent model, no Connection wrapper, no query event: `QueryExecuted` fires only on the wrapper's fetch path (vendor `Connection.php:449`, `:920`), so a `DB::listen` callback never sees the primitive's fetch and cannot forge after it (round 9's poisoned-listener probe ran on the wrapper path; the primitive is below it). Comparisons are BYTE equality of persisted columns — `taxes_applied` JSON strings, encrypted snapshot payloads, base-100 integers, verbatim as stored: no casts, no hydration, no decryption, no normalization (the decoder contract dissolves: there is nothing to decode). The primitive is the LAST word in every refund transaction: it runs after ALL eventful work of the step it verifies — including `issue()`'s return `fresh()` hydration (`InvoiceService.php:530`, round 9's return-hydration escape) and the intent's own `saved` callbacks — and nothing in the step follows it.

- **R0 — replay first, no writes (R2-S3; V2, V4).** Plain read of the intent row by `refundKey`. Exists → the frozen payload must match the request (sale, resolved amount, method, reference, `refundedAt` to the second in UTC); a mismatch → `IdempotencyConflictException::forKey`. A match routes by the row's OWN state, not by stage alone: `Abandoned` → return the stored result, `rectificative` null, terminal, never re-issued (V4); `Refunded` → return the stored result. `Intended` → by the document, when there is one: none → R2; linked document status DRAFT (a phase-1 casualty — the link commits before issuance) → R2 (resume), never R3 (V2); linked document ISSUED with collection open or already PAID-closed → R3 (resume at detection and the flip, §4.5 R3). Replay never consults capacity: the key's own intent already holds it. No row → R1.
- **R1 — the durable intent and its frozen plan (one transaction).** Locks: payment → proforma → original invoice → reservation (§4.0). Under the locks: sale `Settled` required; if the required set grew, restart (R2-S1). Resolve and FREEZE the amount: explicit → positive, sub-cent refused, GROSS; null → the whole remaining refundable (the one predicate of §3), resolved here and stored (R2-S3). Capacity: remaining refundable ≥ resolved amount, else typed refusal with zero writes. **Freeze the plan (V1, R5-1):** choose the path per §D8 — pristine full refund (verbatim per-line components) or complete component allocation per bucket (remaining bucket components = frozen components minus the complete stored plans of prior non-`Abandoned` refunds of this sale; when the resolved gross equals the whole remaining refundable, the plan is TERMINAL and sweeps every bucket's outstanding components to exactly 0, R6-1); store the plan on the row (§6 shape, strict decode). The plan's SOURCE READ — the original's frozen lines and persisted payloads — also goes through the verification primitive (R9-S1: a `retrieved` callback on a hydrated original cannot poison the plan; the persisted bytes are the truth). Write the `sale_refunds` row: `stage = Intended`, full payload + plan, `refund_key` UNIQUE (checked write; a racing key is a conflict, never an adoption). The row is the discriminator (R2-5) and the capacity reservation. **Recovery (R3-7, operational, not policy):** a refund whose issuance is refused by a PROVEN pre-issuance defect — a permanent key conflict measured before any draft existed, or a typed R2a refusal that repeats on replay — may be moved `Intended → Abandoned` by the same key's owner-authorized call, committed under the SAME lock chain (payment → original invoice → reservation) with a locked recheck that the intent still reads `Intended`, `rectificative_invoice_id` is NULL and NO draft carrying the key exists; once a phase-1 draft exists, abandonment is refused — the refund resumes through R2 instead. `Abandoned` releases its capacity, is terminal, and is excluded from `remainingRefundable()`. Recovery and issuance therefore share one lock chain and cannot interleave (R5-S1). Nothing else can abandon an intent, and no intent is ever deleted.
- **R2 — refund-owned issuance.** (a) Fast-fail pre-check (one transaction, locks payment → original invoice → reservation): re-read the intent; if it names an existing document carrying the refund key, that document must ALSO carry the invoice-side intent link — key without link is a typed conflict, zero writes (R3-3); currency pre-check of the same condition R2b enforces (cheap refusal only — it binds nothing, R3-5). (b) The creation call, as its own outermost transaction, creates the durable unnumbered DRAFT with `settlement_operation_key = refundKey`, `sale_refund_id = intent.id`, the lines BUILT FROM THE STORED PLAN (the plan is never recomputed — replay reuses it verbatim, V1) and the original's frozen recipient. **Phase 1 runs under recovery's shared lock chain (payment → original invoice → reservation) and re-checks intent stage `Intended` inside it before inserting** (R6-S: an abandonment that committed between R2a and phase 1 is seen here and refused with zero writes; recovery and creation cannot interleave). **Before the phase-1 commit, the persisted draft's LINES AND HEADER are re-read through the verification primitive and compared with the stored plan for complete equality (R6-2, R7-1, R8-1):** line components AND the header's `taxable_amount`, `total_tax_amount`, `total_amount` against the plan's sums, all by persisted-byte equality. The primitive never hydrates an Eloquent model, so no `retrieved` callback fires on its read — a late-registered listener can neither poison the comparison nor smuggle a mutated clone past it (round 8 probe: a `retrieved` callback cloned the hydrated row, mutated the clone and let a hydrated comparison pass on the clean values). A value-mutating listener that flipped base/tax — on lines (`creating`, round 5-6 probes) or on the header alone (`Invoice::saving`, round 6-7 probes) — causes phase 1 to ROLL BACK ENTIRELY: no draft, no link, the intent stays `Intended` and resumable, and a retry after the listener is removed succeeds; a keyed draft therefore never exists in a plan-mismatched state, and recovery's draft-existence refusal never strands capacity. The intent's `rectificative_invoice_id` is written in the same phase-1 commit (R3-3, R3-4; two-phase contract `InvoiceService.php:261-280`; `issue()` runs as its own outermost call, never inside a stage wrapper). On replay, a draft found by key AND link is resumed through `issue()`; a draft carrying the key WITHOUT the link is not this refund's and is never issued. **Pre-number checks inside issuance (the G7 pattern, R3-5, S1, S2, R5-3):** the config instance that will price the snapshots must resolve the sale's frozen currency; the intent row must read `Intended` under the issuance's own read (an `Abandoned` intent stops issuance mid-flight, S1); the emitted recipient must equal the original's frozen recipient — customer snapshot AND the recipient-derived fiscal context (country, intra-community status, VAT registration): a rectificative's recipient-side fiscal fields are taken from the original's frozen fiscal snapshot, never regenerated from the live profile (S2); and **plan equality (R5-3, R6-3), retained as defense in depth:** the persisted lines are re-read FRESH inside issuance, before the number, and compared for COMPLETE equality with the frozen plan — line count, bucket identity, base, tax, gross and tax entries — AND the fresh HEADER amounts (`taxable_amount`, `total_tax_amount`, `total_amount`) must equal the plan's sums: a header-only mutation through an ordinary `Invoice::saving` listener escapes line comparison (round 6 probe: header `[-101,-20,-121]` issued over correct lines `[-100,-21,-121]`). A drift on any check is a typed refusal BEFORE the correlative is consumed. **Post-save re-verification (R7-2, R8-2, R8-3):** the pre-number comparison runs before Laravel's `updating` callbacks materialize dirty attributes (vendor `Model.php:1534`, `:1548`), so a listener registered after model boot can still mutate the header after the check and before SQL. Issuance therefore re-reads the persisted LINES, HEADER AND RECIPIENT CONTEXT once more AFTER the final save's callbacks — through the verification primitive, as the LAST statement of the issuance transaction, before commit — and compares them with the frozen plan, which since R1 carries the frozen sources (§6: `customer_snapshot.tax_id`, and the recipient-derived fiscal fields — country, intra-community status, VAT registration — from the original's frozen fiscal snapshot; round 8 forged exactly these after the pre-number check). A mismatch rolls back the WHOLE issuance (document, snapshots AND the consumed correlative). **The envelope, declared (R8-2):** one in-transaction seam remains that no in-process design can close — Laravel dispatches `TransactionCommitting` after the transaction closure returns and immediately before PDO commit (vendor `ManagesTransactions.php:49`), and a consumer callback registered there can mutate rows after every in-transaction check; it is the same act as direct SQL forgery of its own database, which no package can prevent. The refund's guarantee against it is DETECTION, with the ordering that makes it deliverable: the intent flip is the refund's LAST write, in its OWN transaction, and it runs only after post-commit detection has passed (§4.5 R3) — so a forgery at the committing seam is caught BEFORE `Refunded` persists, and the intent never reaches it over a forged document. The detection reads through the primitive on the pinned WRITE connection (round 9's read/write PDO split probe: a read-PDO detection would see the clean document while the writer holds the forgery); a mismatch raises `SaleIntegrityException` — the money record never closes, `diagnose()` reports `RefundIncomplete` with the integrity reason, and the consumer's tampering is surfaced instead of silently issued over. The refund never completes over a forged document; that is the boundary. (c) The refund path validates its own lines (per-line identity, frozen rate pairs, per-line sign matching its plan entry's sign, cross-entry Σ `total = −gross`); the ordinary `rectify(items:)` G10 contract is untouched.
- **R3 — money out and completion (rev11: split into three, R9-1, R9-5).** (a) The close (one transaction): locks payment → original invoice → rectificative → reservation. Re-check under the locks: intent stage `Intended`, rectificative link and total, and the rectificative's DOCUMENT status must be ISSUED (V2 — a collection close on an unnumbered DRAFT is a typed refusal, zero writes; `Invoice.php:575-591` does not forbid collection-PAID on document-DRAFT today). The collection axis may be DRAFT (the fresh path — it is born un-stamped, neither the creation stamp `InvoiceService.php:286` nor the adoption stamp `SettlementService.php:359-361` is reused, R3-6) or PAID (a cut-after-R3 replay): DRAFT → `PAID` with `paid_at = refundedAt` through the named checked writer (G17 — the collection axis; `Refunded` is the INTENT's stage, written only at (c), never a collection status, R10-3); PAID → idempotent completion of the same close; anything else is a typed conflict, zero writes. The primitive then re-verifies the persisted document (components, header, frozen sources) as the LAST statement before commit — an `updating` listener conditioned on collection-PAID mutates after the model guard and before SQL (round 8 probe) and the primitive sees the mutated persisted row and rolls the whole transaction back (R8-4): intent stays `Intended`, nothing persisted. (b) Post-commit detection: the primitive re-reads the COMMITTED document on the write connection; a mismatch raises `SaleIntegrityException` and the flip never runs — a `TransactionCommitting` callback conditioned on the close cannot deliver a forged `Refunded` (round 9 probe: with the old ordering it could; with the split it cannot). (c) The intent flip, in its OWN short transaction after detection passes: `stage = Refunded`, nothing else — and the PRIMITIVE then re-verifies the persisted document as that transaction's LAST statement, before its commit (R9-5, R10-2): the intent's own `saved` callbacks fire on the save, and a listener there can forge the document AFTER detection — round 10 measured both orderings (literal flip-only: `fired=1 base=-101 intent=Refunded`; flip-then-primitive: `base=-100 intent=Intended`, refused). The flip transaction commits only over a document the primitive just re-verified. A cut between (a) and (c) is resumable by key (R0). Final state re-read (one refund row `Refunded`, rectificative ISSUED + PAID, original allocation ACTIVE, payment POSTED, zero `credit_movements`). Intent `stage = Refunded`; re-read before commit through the hydration-free raw query (R8-4): the close is the LAST fiscal write of the refund — after its save, the raw re-read re-verifies the rectificative's components, header AND recipient context against the frozen plan before the intent flips (an `updating` listener conditioned on collection-PAID mutates after the model guard and before SQL — round 8 probe — and the raw re-read sees the mutated row and refuses: full rollback, intent stays `Intended`, nothing persisted). Only then: rectificative ISSUED + PAID, exactly one refund row, original allocation ACTIVE, payment POSTED, zero `credit_movements`.

Cuts: after R1 → an intent reserving capacity, resumed by key; after R2 → an issued rectificative owned by the intent from creation, collection open, `diagnose()` reports `SaleIntegrityReason::RefundIncomplete`, resumed by key. A rectificative issued outside `refund()` is never adopted (no intent link, R3-3) and the ordinary package behaviour is untouched. A refund whose intent exists and whose capacity was later claimed by ANOTHER key's refund stays resumable: its capacity was reserved at R1 and the re-read under the locks proves it.

## 9. G2 — buyer-based tax classification (answer, not code)

**It belongs to the package.** Classifying the operation by buyer (domestic, intra-EU B2B with reverse charge, intra-EU B2C under OSS, outside the EU) is fiscal domain logic that must give the same answer for every consumer; the package already owns tax calculation (`TaxCalculationService`), the reverse-charge coherence guard (`ReverseChargeWithTaxException`, AID-929) and consumes `lararoi` for VAT-number verification. An app computing it would duplicate the domain in each consumer.

**State today, measured:** the tax group of a line is the explicit `tax_group_id` in the item data, else the article's (`TaxCalculationService.php:98-100`); the default VAT strategy applies the selected group's rates and does not classify by buyer (`VatCalculationStrategy.php:29`); conversion keeps the proforma's frozen tax amounts verbatim (`InvoiceService.php:1229`); `DestinationVatService` exists, is not wired into emission and still returns `float` rates (against ADR-009); `is_roi_taxed` is decided by the caller.

**Consequence:** not part of 9.6.0. It needs its own ticket, spec and gate (it changes tax amounts — a semantic change of emission). Until then the consumer sells only in Spain and passes `is_roi_taxed = false`.
