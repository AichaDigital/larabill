## 1. Main flow

Accepted proforma → correct payment → top-up (voucher-sale) invoice → balance → consumption.

1. **`sellVoucher()`**, keyed. The key identifies the payment event **as declared by the consumer** (card: its payment-intent id; transfer: the consumer's own durable identifier of that credit). larabill does not assert that a composed bank reference uniquely identifies a movement and cannot detect one external receipt presented under two different keys — a consumer residual (§6).
   - **S0+S1 — claim and record, one transaction.** Under the proforma lock: proforma in force (DRAFT or SENT), unclaimed, exact amount and currency → a **sale claim** is written (proforma id, payment id, stage `pending`, and the **frozen expectation**: amount, currency, billable user) and the payment is recorded POSTED with no allocation and **no credit entry** (recorded, not spendable). This needs an internal recording mode: today `receive()` always creates the remainder entry (`PaymentService.php:600-617`). Anything else → named refusal, zero writes (§2).
   - **S2 — convert.** Under payment → proforma locks (the global order; measured on MySQL 8.4 during implementation: with the proforma lock alone, a concurrent `reverse()` at PENDING — which holds the payment lock while it releases the claim — let S2 convert and then die on the `sale_claim_id` foreign key), re-checking that the payment is still POSTED and the claim still exists; then the existing `convertProformaToInvoice()`; the resulting PREPARED invoice gets `sale_claim_id` in the same transaction.
   - **S3a — revalidate and issue.** Stage `pending` → `issuing` in a short write under payment → proforma → invoice locks, re-checking that the payment is POSTED and unreversed and that **the invoice still matches the frozen expectation** (§1a rule 4); then the existing `issue()`, which writes the sale mark on the claim inside its own transaction (§3), with the optional hook.
   - **S3b — settle and back, one transaction** under payment → proforma → invoice locks, after re-checking the expectation and that the invoice has **no active allocation** (§1a rule 2):
     1. the invoice's collection status DRAFT → PENDING (`issue()` leaves DRAFT, `InvoiceService.php:443-447`; `recomputeCollectionState()` returns early on DRAFT, `Invoice.php:819-826`);
     2. **one allocation row** payment → sale invoice for the full amount (ACTIVE, `applied_at` = the payment's `paid_at`), written by the package under the locks — not through `receive()`;
     3. `recomputeCollectionState()` (`Invoice.php:838-853`), **then the row is re-read and must be PAID with `paid_at` set — otherwise S3b throws and the transaction rolls back** (round 13 P1-4: `save()` returns `false` without throwing when a `saving` listener vetoes, `vendor/laravel/framework/src/Illuminate/Database/Eloquent/Model.php:1415-1425`); `amountPaid()` = 100 €, `amountOutstanding()` = 0 € (`Invoice.php:754-772`);
     4. **one voucher entry** (kind `TOP_UP`, 100 €) with `grouped_payment_id` = the payment and `sale_invoice_id` = the invoice; stage → `backed`.
   - **Same key:** returns the same result, never a second allocation or entry; same key with another payload → refused.
2. **Why one payment carries both the allocation and the entry (B5, measured):** no package invariant asserts «allocations + entries ≤ payment amount»; the only payment-level sums are `unallocatedAmount()` and `availableCredit()` (`GroupedPayment.php:115-142`). The one path that would apply the same money again is `allocate()`, funded from `availableCredit()`/`consumeEntries()` (`PaymentService.php:293-299, 534`); the voucher filter closes it. The entry stays linked to its payment (traceability; `reverse()`'s active-exit guard, `PaymentService.php:351-361`; `annul()`'s active-allocation guard, `SettlementService.php:488-492`). `unallocatedAmount()` of the sale payment reads 0 while its voucher is inside `balance()`: the two figures are not independent receipts and no reader may add them (no package PDF or report does today; a census is pending at implementation).
3. **Balance:** `breakdown()` adds «voucher-backed»; for data without vouchers the 9.1 figures are unchanged; consumable + advance-backed + voucher-backed = `balance()`.
4. **Protection of voucher balance — entries with `sale_invoice_id` only, so 9.1 data is untouched:** `apply()` and `refund()` (through `plan()`, `CustomerCredit.php:466-476`) **and `inferCurrency()`** (`CustomerCredit.php:376-399`, round 13 P2) never select a voucher entry; `allocate()` (through `availableCredit()` and `consumeEntries()`) never funds from one; `linkAdvanceInvoice()` refuses an entry with `sale_invoice_id`; `annul()` refuses a voucher-sale invoice that is unbacked, has live balance or has consumptions.
5. **`consumeVoucher()`**, keyed: a consumption exit on the voucher entry with a service reference, all or nothing, never below zero, plus the data of a receipt that is **not an invoice**.
6. **Proforma cancellation under lock:** refuses a claimed, converting or converted proforma; a closed proforma is never reopened.

## 1a. The sale-integrity boundary (operator 20:01; rounds 12 B1/B2 and 13 P1-1..P1-4)

One boundary, applied to **claimed rows only** (a proforma with a sale claim, the invoice carrying `sale_claim_id`, the payment referenced by the claim). Rows without a claim behave exactly as in 9.1.

| # | Rule | Enforced where | Error |
| -- | -- | -- | -- |
| 1 | **No fiscal-content edit during the claim.** From S1 until `backed`, writes **and deletes** to the claimed proforma, its PREPARED invoice and their lines are refused — enforced on the **model events** `saving` **and `deleting`** of `Invoice` and `InvoiceItem` (today both guards apply only to immutable or ISSUED rows, `Invoice.php:528-532`, `InvoiceItem.php:79-93`; the converted invoice is born with `is_immutable = false`, `InvoiceService.php:947`). `convertProformaToInvoice()`, `convertAndIssue()`, `issue()` and `cancelPreparedInvoice()` refuse a claimed row unless invoked by `sellVoucher()` (`InvoiceService.php:534-546, 583-601, 475-505`). | `saving` + `deleting` guards on both models; the four service entry points | `SaleClaimedException` |
| 2 | **No collection by another path — enforced on the allocation writers, not on their callers.** The three places that create or reverse an allocation refuse an invoice with `sale_claim_id`: `PaymentService::process()` (shared by `receive()` and `GroupedPaymentService::register()`, `GroupedPaymentService.php:102`; today PENDING is payable and it would allocate, `PaymentService.php:150-177`), `CustomerCredit::apply()` (creates allocations itself through `Ledger::createAllocation()`, `CustomerCredit.php:283`, `Ledger.php:175-186`), and `reverseAllocation()` (would mint a SURPLUS entry from the sale allocation, `:452-470`); `allocate()` is covered through the voucher filter and the `process()` target check. The deprecated collection setters `markAsPaidViaGroupedPayment()` / `restoreStateViaGroupedPaymentReversal()` (`Invoice.php:728-745`) are **not** intercepted — a declared, unsupported consumer mutation; what the integrity reads detect of their effect is stated in §3, no more. | `process()`, `apply()`, `reverseAllocation()` | `SaleClaimedException` |
| 3 | **No balance without the confirmed S3b tuple.** S3b commits only if, re-read inside the same transaction after its writes, the **whole tuple** holds: exactly one ACTIVE allocation on the invoice, from the claimed payment, for the frozen amount; the invoice PAID with `paid_at` = the payment's `paid_at`; exactly one voucher entry linked to that payment and that invoice for the frozen amount; the claim at stage `backed`. Any other persisted outcome throws before commit (`save()` may return `false` without throwing and `create()` ignores it — `Model.php:1415-1425`, `Builder.php:1277-1281`). | S3b | `SaleIntegrityException` |
| 4 | **Revalidation before numbering.** S3a compares the invoice with the claim's frozen expectation — total amount, currency, billable user — **and the payment with the same expectation — POSTED, unreversed, same amount, currency and `billable_user_id`** (fillable, `GroupedPayment.php:48-50`) — before `issue()`; S3b repeats the comparison. A mismatch refuses and the stage is not advanced. | S3a, S3b | `SaleIntegrityException` |
| 5 | **Reversal coordinated with the sale.** `reverse()` on the claimed payment is allowed only at stage `pending` and releases the claim; **if S2 already ran, the PREPARED invoice is cancelled through `cancelPreparedInvoice()` in the same operation** (document CANCELLED, proforma back to DRAFT, `InvoiceService.php:475-505`); refused at `issuing` and `backed`. | `reverse()` | `SaleStageException` |

Exception classes are the names clientes catches by type; their final names are an implementation detail, the set is not.

## 1b. Declared limit of the guarantee (operator 06:34, 2026-11-02 — implementation gate rounds 7–9)

What §1a guarantees, and against what:

- **Covered:** the package's own public paths (`sellVoucher()`, `consumeVoucher()`, `cancelProforma()`, `PaymentService::receive()/process()/allocate()/reverse()`, `CustomerCredit::apply()/refund()`, `InvoiceService::issue()/convertProformaToInvoice()/cancelPreparedInvoice()`, `SettlementService::annul()`, the model guards on `Invoice`/`InvoiceItem`), their concurrent use (the 13 fork cases, each with a named mutation and its result: eleven red, F7 and F11 red only under a double mutation because two independent layers refuse, F4 not sensitive by construction, F9's window unreachable by a plain race and covered by F9b), and the ordinary failures of the optional issuance hook (a throwing hook rolls the issuance back; a hook that writes to a claimed row is refused; a hook that changes the funding payment's amount, currency, payer or posted state, or the invoice's collection state, makes the issuance roll back — `reference`, `method` and `paid_at` are not compared there; a hook that never ran leaves a RESUMABLE state — the re-call completes it, and it is identifiable through the claim at stage ISSUING — but not a listed one: the recovery reads list sales awaiting balance and incoherent sales, and the ISSUING/PREPARED crash window is neither).
- **Not covered (declared, not promised):** code the consumer runs *inside larabill's own process and transactions* — model listeners registered on larabill's models, raw SQL or query-builder writes — when that code rewrites a larabill row between one of the package's checks and its commit. Such a write is the consumer's own; it can equally rewrite during a re-read, so no amount of re-reading closes it. S3b re-reads the payment, invoice, allocation and entry, and a consumption re-reads its exits, under their locks before committing, which catches some faults of such code; S2 and an issuance without a bound hook do not re-read after their writes. That is a courtesy where it exists, not a guarantee anywhere.
- **Never inside the guarantee:** external writes to the database (another process, another connection, a migration, a DBA). The limit above is not a protection against them and is not presented as one; §3's recovery reads are what identify a sale those writes left incoherent.

Rounds 7, 8 and 9 of the implementation gate each returned only permutations of the «not covered» class (2, 2 and 5 findings); the five of round 9 are left as named, unfixed instances of a declared limit, not as open defects.

## 2. Money that is not a sale — refused, not managed

`sellVoucher()` writes nothing and returns a named outcome for: an amount or currency different from the proforma's; a cancelled proforma; a proforma already claimed, converting or converted. That money never becomes balance and never produces an invoice; its recording and refund are administration and accounting incidents outside larabill in this ticket (operator 19:22). A closed proforma's payment is refunded; continuation is a new proforma and a new payment; the express application of the old receipt is deferred (18:21). A failure between S1 and S3a leaves the claim at `pending`: the payment can be undone with `reverse()` and what happens to the money is administration's.

## 3. Fiscal obligations, sale mark and recovery

- **Without the hook:** `issue()` stamps `NOT_REQUIRED` as today; the consumer registers after SOLD as for any invoice. A crash between SOLD and that registration leaves an issued invoice without a local record: **the consumer owns that reconciliation** (duplicate `register()` throws, `RegistryManager::assertNoRootRegistration`). larabill makes no fiscal call.
- **With the optional hook** `VoucherSaleIssuanceHookContract::afterIssue(Invoice)`: inside `issue()`'s transaction, same connection, outermost transaction; it receives an `ISSUED`, numbered invoice; `is_immutable` is not promised either way.
- **Sale mark:** `issue()`, when invoked by `sellVoucher()`, writes the mark «issued by the sale» on the claim in its own transaction, hook or not; a separate flag records that the hook ran. Same-key re-call: mark present → S3b, no second hook call.
- **An incoherent sale is identifiable and returns a clear error — no guarantee that a re-call completes it.** A re-call re-runs the checks of §1a on every stage. **What identifies the sale:** the invoice's `sale_claim_id`; the claim (proforma id, payment id, stage, frozen expectation, mark, hook flag, instants); the payment's status, amount, currency and payer; the invoice's document status, collection status, `paid_at` and active allocations. **The error:** `SaleIntegrityException` with one reason per detected incoherence — `paymentNotPosted`, `amountDrift`, `currencyDrift`, `payerDrift`, `contentDrift`, `competingAllocation`, `collectionStateUnexpected`, `claimMissing`, `documentAxisIncoherent`, `markMissing` — zero writes, no fiscal call; the reason is readable from the exception. **The mark by document state (round 14 B5):** a PREPARED invoice without the mark at stage `issuing` is the **normal** crash window between the stage write and `issue()`; the re-call resumes it. `markMissing` applies only to an **ISSUED** invoice without the mark. **Visible recovery:** two reads over the **invoices** that carry `sale_claim_id` (not over the claim, so a lost claim is listed too), each row carrying invoice id, proforma id, payment id, stage and reason: `salesAwaitingBalance()` (issued, marked, no voucher entry — completed by the re-call when every check passes) and `salesIncoherent()`, whose predicates are: claim missing; frozen expectation ≠ invoice (amount, currency, billable user); frozen expectation ≠ payment (amount, currency, payer) or payment not POSTED; ISSUED without mark; active allocations ≠ exactly one from the claimed payment for the frozen amount when stage is `backed` (or any when earlier); collection status or `paid_at` ≠ what the stage implies (`backed` ⇒ PAID with the payment's `paid_at`); voucher entry count ≠ what the stage implies. **Not listed by either read:** a claim at `pending` or `issuing` whose payment changed before S2 (no invoice exists yet) — it surfaces as a named refusal on the re-call, and as a claim with no invoice in the claim table. What a person does with an incoherent sale is outside this delivery (no abandon operation, no administrative flow; operator 19:22).
- **Corruption larabill cannot detect:** a write outside the package that keeps every field mutually coherent — for example a claim, invoice and payment rewritten together to a new amount, a forged mark, or a sale invoice whose `sale_claim_id` was set to NULL (it then looks like an ordinary issued invoice). The reads and checks above see disagreements between the pieces, not a consistent rewrite of all of them.
- **Retries (round 11 B2):** `issue()` runs its transaction with 3 attempts (`InvoiceService.php:450-451`); the hook may run up to three times in one call and must be retry-safe; same-connection writes roll back with each failed attempt. The other retry owner is the consumer, re-calling with the same key.
- **Not undone by a rollback (round 11 B3/B4):** logs; events and synchronous listeners except their same-connection writes; writes on another connection; anything external. `afterCommit` callbacks run after the root commit; if one fails, the database is committed and **the external outcome is unknown** — the consumer reconciles; larabill does not guarantee delivery.

7. **Refusals:** wrong amount, wrong currency, cancelled proforma, claimed/converting/converted proforma each give their named outcome and write nothing.

13. **Identification (round 13 P1-3, round 14 B5/B6):** for each incoherence — claim deleted, collection status set to CANCELLED through the deprecated setter, `paid_at` changed through the setter while PAID, competing allocation inserted directly with the expected status restored, mark removed from an ISSUED invoice, document axis altered by a direct write — the re-call returns the matching `SaleIntegrityException` reason with zero writes, and `salesIncoherent()` lists the invoice with proforma id, payment id, stage and reason; `salesAwaitingBalance()` lists only the normal crash window. **A crash fixture right after the `issuing` stage write (PREPARED, no mark) is resumed by the re-call and listed by neither read as incoherent.** Mutation: a read defined over the claim instead of the invoice fails the deleted-claim case; `markMissing` applied to PREPARED fails the crash fixture.

15. **Consumption:** all or nothing, never below zero, idempotent by key; a consumed voucher's payment cannot be reversed.
16. **Concurrency on MySQL 8.4 and MariaDB 11.4:** two sales with the same key; two different keys on one proforma; sale against `convertAndIssue()`, against an edit of the PREPARED invoice, against `receive()` on the sale invoice, against `cancelPreparedInvoice()`, against `reverse()` and against proforma cancellation; two consumptions of one balance; consumption against annulment.
