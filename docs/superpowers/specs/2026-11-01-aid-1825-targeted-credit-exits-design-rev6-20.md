## 3. Diagnosis, measured on `2785375` [V]

- **D1 — a conversion-born invoice never reaches PAID.** `Invoice::recomputeCollectionState()` (`src/Models/Invoice.php:815`) returns early when the document is not issued, when the collection status is DRAFT, or when no allocation rows exist. The voucher-sale invoice has no allocation rows (money cannot be allocated to a proforma, see D3), so nothing marks it collected; it stays at collection DRAFT and is out of `outstanding()` only by accident (DRAFT is not a payable status, `src/Services/Payments/Ledger.php:142`).
- **D2 — a converted invoice cannot be linked as an entry's backing.** `CreditMovement::linkAdvanceInvoice()` (`src/Models/CreditMovement.php:231-252`) accepts only an unnumbered DRAFT of kind INVOICE and refuses anything else (`::advanceNotDraft`, `::advanceNotAnInvoice`); the conversion yields a PREPARED document and `issue()` seals it. The only link column is `advance_invoice_id` (`database/migrations/2026_10_24_000001_add_allocations_and_credit_movements.php:264`), whose meaning today is "VAT declared on an advance; exits produce deduction lines" (AID-1778, 9.1 `breakdown()`).
- **D3 — money never lands on the proforma.** `Ledger::assertPayable()` refuses the PROFORMA serie (`src/Services/Payments/Ledger.php:107`; also `src/Services/GroupedPaymentService.php:167`). A receipt for a top-up is therefore recorded with zero allocations and becomes a **consumable** TOP_UP entry (`recordRemainder()`, `src/Services/PaymentService.php:600-616`) — exactly what a mismatched receipt must **not** become (21:36).
- **D4 — no exact-amount check and no proforma reference on the receipt.** `receive()` with zero allocations accepts any amount; the ledger has no link between an entry and a proforma; the exactness and the conversion are today two unrelated calls of the consumer.
- **D5 — untargeted plans.** `CustomerCredit::plan()` (`src/Services/CustomerCredit.php:466-511`) selects active entries by customer and currency and never reads the backing link; shared by `apply()` (`:244`) and `refund()` (`:315`); `PaymentService::allocate()` funds from the payment's own entries without a filter. There is no exit kind that reduces a balance against a service without a payment allocation and without a deduction line.

A proforma whose line is base-first may not land on the round gross the customer expects to pay.
- **D10 — no expiry, no reactivation.** No code path expires a proforma; `InvoiceStatus` offers CANCELLED and CONVERTED as a proforma's terminal states and nothing moves CANCELLED back to DRAFT.

A proforma is cancelled today by the consumer writing `status = CANCELLED` on the collection axis, with no instant, actor or reason anywhere; `assertStatusIsBirthEligible()` only forbids being born CANCELLED (`src/Services/InvoiceService.php:759-768`). The «two axes» of rev 6.1 gap 3 describe a **fiscal** draft cancelled directly (the `cancelPreparedInvoice()` docblock, `:457-471`), not a proforma. No invoice audit or event table exists in the package.
- **D11 — what already serves the flow unchanged.** Conversion with verbatim line clone and idempotency; `issue()` numbering and sealing; the payment idempotency key and replay; the append-only ledger, bands and `reversed_at`; `breakdown()` and `CreditEntryRemainder`; the single-rate and reverse-charge refusals of the advance builder; `rectificativeLines()` pro-rata; `SettlementService::rectify()`.

## 5. Design [P unless marked]

### 5.1 Fiscal backing of an entry

An entry (kinds SURPLUS and TOP_UP) carries **one** fiscal backing: none (consumable surplus), **advance** (an issued advance invoice declared the VAT; exits produce deduction or rectificative lines — AID-1778 / AID-1815), or **voucher sale** (the issued voucher-sale invoice declared the VAT; exits are consumptions documented by a receipt). The backing is set once, at the entry's birth (voucher) or at the link (advance), and survives reversal. Derived classification of the balance: **consumable** (no backing) ⊎ **voucher-backed** ⊎ **advance-backed**; the three sum to `balance()`. **Representation (rev 6.11, gate V7) [P]:** the existing `advance_invoice_id` stays the **authoritative** mark of an advance backing — every 9.1 advance-backed entry keeps meaning exactly what it means today, with no backfill and no reinterpretation; the voucher backing is a **second** nullable link, `sale_invoice_id`; both NULL = consumable; both non-NULL is refused by **the two package writers of links** — `linkAdvanceInvoice()` and S3b — under their locks, and by a database CHECK **only if both engines accept its DDL** (rev 6.18, gate round 7 V1: rev 6.17 said «by the model», but `linkAdvanceInvoice()` writes through the query builder and bypasses the model; rev 6.19, operator 12:36: the foreign key is not changed, and without the CHECK a write outside the package's methods that sets both links is not covered — direct SQL, the query builder, or Eloquent mass assignment by a consumer on the model's fillable attributes (`advance_invoice_id`, `grouped_payment_id`, `consumes_movement_id`, `reversed_at`, `CreditMovement.php:64-68`; the new `sale_invoice_id` and `deduction_invoice_id` are proposed **not** fillable) — §5.13 «Rule 1 revised»). No `backing_kind` column is proposed any more (rev 6.10's «NULL = none» would have reclassified legacy advance money as consumable, defeating G2).

Invariants, each checked under the entry's payment lock in the write that could break it:

### 5.2 Sale-with-balance primitive (the one call of step 3)


Behaviour:

1. **Two kinds of precondition (rev 6.6 errata).** (i) **Malformed call → zero writes:** unknown proforma; a document that is not a PROFORMA; a proforma of another customer; a non-positive amount; a missing idempotency key; a proforma whose line does not resolve to one fiscal dimension (G6 — a design error of the consumer, not money to keep; this zero-write treatment relies on G6 being enforced at the proforma's creation, §2 step 1, so such a proforma can never have been accepted or paid — if G6 were enforced only at sale time, this item would be money received and would move to (ii)). (ii) **Money received for an identified proforma → always recorded by S1, then classified:** proforma in force (DRAFT or SENT) and amount equal to the total in the same currency → sale (steps 3–4); proforma closed **unpaid** (CANCELLED) → controlled receipt `proforma_not_in_force` (a late payment); proforma CONVERTING or CONVERTED — a sale already in progress or done for another payment — → outcome **DUPLICATE**, **zero writes** (§5.2b; the single carve-out, by the operator's word of 00:09, to «a received payment is never refused unrecorded»); amount different from the total → `amount_mismatch`; currency different from the proforma's → `currency_mismatch` [P, §5.3]; conversion refused at S2 → `proforma_not_convertible`. A received payment is never refused unrecorded (21:36: «queda controlado») — **except** a duplicate (§5.2b), which larabill does not record at all (00:09).
2. **Idempotency (G9):** the payment key is the operation key. A re-call with the same key and the same payload (proforma id, amount, currency, method, received_at to the second) returns the **recorded outcome**: it resumes an unfinished sale (step 4) or replays a finished one, SOLD or CONTROLLED alike; a different payload is `IdempotencyConflictException::forKey()` (existing replay semantics of `receive()`, `src/Services/PaymentService.php:241-250`, extended to the proforma id). **Rev 6.1 (gap 1), narrowed at 22:31:** a CONTROLLED outcome is never turned into a sale by re-calling `sellVoucher()` with the same payment; the replay decision reads the recorded outcome, never the proforma's current state. A controlled receipt for a closed proforma is refunded, or applied to a **new** accepted proforma by the express act of §5.3c (D2, 23:25) — never by a replay.
3. **Exactness (G5):** `amount == proforma.total_amount` to the cent. Otherwise → **controlled receipt** (§5.3, reason `amount_mismatch`) and return CONTROLLED; the proforma is not touched (14:43).
4. **Sale, resumable, lock order payment → proforma → invoice** (rev 5 D8, kept as the proposed order). **Rev 6.1 (gaps 2 and 5): the step structure below is the proposed behaviour; the transactional boundaries, the retry behaviour and the recovery of each failure mode are pending proof [T] and are not closed by this text.**
   - **S0+S1 — classify under the target's lock, then record, in one transaction (rev 6.11, gate V2).** The transaction takes the **proforma row lock first**, reads its state, and classifies: DRAFT/SENT and not claimed → this payment **claims** the sale (`sale_claim`: payment id, stage «sale pending», written on the proforma) and is recorded POSTED with zero allocations and **no entry**; CANCELLED → recorded as a controlled receipt `proforma_not_in_force`; CONVERTING / CONVERTED / already claimed by another payment (unless this payment is the earmark difference, §5.3c) → **DUPLICATE, zero writes** — the transaction rolls back having written nothing. Because the classification and the durable write share the lock, two different keys cannot both record against one proforma: the first claims, the second is DUPLICATE. Whatever happens next for a claimed sale, the money is recorded. Between S1 and S3b it is recorded but **not spendable** (no entry exists).

### 5.2b Duplicate payment versus repeated notification (rev 6.10) — [B] 00:09, mechanism [P]

- **Business decision [B]:** a second **real** payment for an operation already paid or being invoiced creates no invoice, no voucher and no balance in larabill; administration contacts the customer and handles the refund; the documentary record of that money and of its refund belongs to administration or accounting, not to larabill's fiscal operations; a **retry** of the same payment is never recorded as a second receipt; the correct payment and its invoice are never modified.
- **What the idempotency key identifies [V + P]:** the key names **one payment event as the consumer identified it** — not a bank movement by itself. [V] today it is a string the consumer supplies (or `ReceiveRequest::deriveIdempotencyKey()` builds from payer, currency, amount, date, method, reference, credit kind and allocation map, `src/Services/Payments/ReceiveRequest.php:50`), and the replay compares the recorded payload (method, reference, allocation map, `received_at` to the second, `PaymentService.php:218-250`). Same key + same payload → **replay** (nothing new is recorded, the recorded outcome is returned); same key + different payload → **conflict**; different key → a **new event** as far as the package can tell. **A different key does not prove two bank movements**: it proves that the consumer named two events. Whether two events are two movements of money is the integrator's check, below.
- **What the integrator must check before calling with a new key [C], and the manual-transfer rule [B] 00:32 (rev 6.11, gate V1 and Q3):** card — the gateway identifies the charge (PaymentIntent / charge id); every webhook redelivery of the same charge carries the same id → same key; a second charge id is a second movement (measured in clientes: `SettleInvoicePayment.php:48`, `:115`). Transfer — **no tuple of bank data is an identity unless demonstrated**: value date + amount + reference, even with the payer account, is **not unique** (two identical monthly fees from one payer on one day), and the only safe discriminator, a bank-provided movement id, is not ingested by clientes (no automatic bank access, operator's rule); rev 6.10's wording that «the bank line identifies the movement» is withdrawn. The rule the operator decided: when the system detects a **possible match** (same payer, amount, currency, date window, reference) but cannot identify the movement reliably, it **warns administration and a person decides after checking the bank statement**; the system **never merges** two notifications into one by heuristic and **never starts a refund** because date, amount and reference coincide. The person's answer is one of two: «same movement» → nothing is recorded again (the consumer does not call, or calls with the original key, which replays); «second real receipt» → the consumer calls with a new key and the 00:09 rule applies (DUPLICATE if the target is already funded; otherwise the ordinary classification). Package support [P]: a read `possibleMatches(billableUserId, amount, currency, receivedAt window, reference)` that lists recorded payments and controlled receipts coinciding on those fields, for the consumer's warning — a listing, never a decision; the sale and receive primitives themselves compare only keys and payloads.
- **Classification in larabill [P] (rev 6.11, gate V9: a target must be declared — `ReceiveRequest` gains a nullable `targetInvoiceId`, part of the replay payload and persisted on the payment; today it has no such field, `src/Services/Payments/ReceiveRequest.php:33-45`):** the receive paths (`sellVoucher()`, `receive()` with allocations, or `receive()` with a declared target invoice and zero allocations) refuse as DUPLICATE, with **zero writes**, under the target's lock (S0), a new-key payment whose target is: a proforma CONVERTING or CONVERTED (its sale is funded by another payment); an invoice already PAID, or fully covered by active allocations, or whose outstanding is zero ([V] an allocation to a PAID invoice is already refused today by `Ledger::assertPayable()`, `Ledger.php:88-118`; the gap today is the zero-allocation path, which creates consumable credit through `recordRemainder()`). The refusal is a named outcome (`DUPLICATE`) and a domain exception, **not** a generic error.
- **What is lost under a wrong identity (gate Q3), stated so no one reads DUPLICATE as a cure:** two real transfers treated as one retry → the second movement never reaches the DUPLICATE outcome and its refund incident is lost from the workflow; one retry treated as two movements → a refund not owed is routed, or on another target the money is recorded twice. Zero writes cure neither; only the person's check of the statement does (00:32).
- **How the incident reaches administration when larabill writes no row [P/C]:** the DUPLICATE outcome carries everything administration needs and nothing is persisted by the package: the payment data as received (amount, currency, method, reference, received_at, key), the target it was declared for (proforma or invoice) and the existing payment that already funds that target (id, key, amount, date). The **consumer** records that outcome in its own incident channel (its staff queue or ticket, as rev16 decides) and administration handles the refund outside larabill; accounting keeps the record. The package guarantees only that the refusal is deterministic (the same call always yields DUPLICATE) and that the correct payment and invoice are untouched.
- **Not a duplicate:** a mismatched amount for a proforma in force (`amount_mismatch`), money for a cancelled unpaid proforma (`proforma_not_in_force`), an overpayment of a payable invoice (§5.3b) — those are controlled receipts, recorded.

### 5.3 Controlled receipt (mismatched amount)

- **What it is [B]:** money received for a top-up whose amount differs from the proforma's; «queda controlado y pendiente de devolución».
- **Behaviour:** recorded as a POSTED payment with zero allocations and **no credit entry** — hence spendable by nothing (G4): no plan can select what does not exist in the ledger, and `assertPayable()` already keeps it off the proforma. It carries the proforma id it was meant for and **one of six reasons (rev 6.1, gap 4; fourth added by the 22:36 errata; fifth `invoice_overpayment` in rev 6.8; sixth `application_excess` in rev 6.9):** `amount_mismatch` (the amount differs from the proforma total), `currency_mismatch` [P] (the payment arrived in a currency other than the proforma's — a different amount by nature; whether the consumer must normalise before calling or the package records it this way is the proposal: record it, never refuse money unrecorded), `proforma_not_in_force` (the proforma was CANCELLED, unpaid, when the money arrived — a late payment; **rev 6.10:** CONVERTING and CONVERTED are no longer here — a payment for them is a DUPLICATE, §5.2b, including a second real payment arriving between S2 and S3a of the same sale), `proforma_not_convertible` (the conversion was refused, §5.2 S2); `invoice_overpayment` (§5.3b); `application_excess` (the part of an applied receipt above the new proforma's total, §5.3c). A closed set: the consumer's screens enumerate these six and nothing else. It is listed by a new read (`controlledReceipts()` per customer, bounded, with reason) so the consumer's screens can act on it (C8). Its exits depend on the reason (review below): **refund**, recorded by the consumer with the refund means and confirmation date (the payment is marked refunded; no fiscal document, nothing was sold — the fiscal reading of this case is the consumer's, the package records), where a decision provides for it; **reversal**, for every reason, when the receipt itself was a recording error; **resolution into a sale**, only where the review below keeps it as a candidate.
- **Resolution of controlled receipts — a separate case, outside the normal flow ([B] 22:40).** The normal flow of §2 (proforma → exact payment → conversion → balance → consumptions) does not depend on anything below; a controlled receipt is money recorded and kept under control, and that is all the normal flow needs to know. **Review of the reasons before withdrawing `resolveControlledReceipt()` (rev 6.6, operator order 22:34, confirmed 22:40):** the question is, per reason, whether the recorded payment can still become the sale it was meant for, and who decides.

- **Shape:** whether this is a payment status, a dedicated table, or a flag on `grouped_payments` is a technical choice (§12). The behaviour above is the contract.

### 5.3b Overpayment of an ordinary invoice (rev 6.8) — [B] 23:02, mechanism [P], proofs [T]

- **Business decision [B]:** for clientes, the amount received above the invoice's outstanding is a controlled receipt for refund; never automatically a top-up nor consumable balance. The package supports other per-consumer policies and imposes none (C9). The technical shape and any later fiscal treatment are pending design — what follows is the proposal.
- **Today [V]:** `receive()` with allocations records the remainder through `recordRemainder()` as **one consumable entry** of the requested kind (`src/Services/PaymentService.php:600-616`); `ReceiveRequest` already carries that kind (`creditKind`, part of the derived key, `src/Services/Payments/ReceiveRequest.php:42-50`); `GroupedPayment::unallocatedAmount()` measures the excess from the allocations (`src/Models/GroupedPayment.php:115`); the replay compares the kind only when an entry exists (`PaymentService.php:241-250`).
- **Proposal [P] — a remainder policy chosen per call:** `ReceiveRequest` gains `remainder: RemainderPolicy` with two values — `Credit` (today's behaviour: one consumable entry of `creditKind`; the default, so no 9.1 caller changes) and `Controlled` (no entry is created; the excess is recorded as a **controlled receipt** of the payment with reason `invoice_overpayment`, the invoice ids it exceeded as its subject). The consumer's policy picks the value (clientes v1: `Controlled`; a deferred-mode consumer: `Credit`). The allocations themselves are unchanged: the invoice is paid exactly as today, the collection state moves as today.
- **Replay and derived identity [P] (gate V10, round 3 V6: today `replayMatches()` knows no policy and compares the kind only when an entry exists, `PaymentService.php:241-250`; the derived key excludes it, `ReceiveRequest.php:185-201`; `receive()` lets the caller omit the key, `PaymentService.php:64-77`):** the policy and the declared target are part of the replay payload; the **derived** key includes them **only when non-default** (`Controlled`, or a target declared), so every existing caller's key is byte-identical to today's — compatibility stated, not assumed, and tested. The policy is part of the payload: the recorded receipt (or its absence) is compared on a same-key re-call — same policy → replay; a different policy → `IdempotencyConflictException::forKey()`. With `Controlled`, the existing «compare the kind only when an entry exists» rule is replaced by comparing the recorded policy, because no entry exists.
- **Spendable by nothing [P → G4]:** with `Controlled` no ledger entry exists, so no plan of `apply()`, `inferCurrency()`, `allocate()` or `refund()` can reach the excess, by construction; `availableCredit()` of the payment is zero; `breakdown()` does not show it (it is not balance); `GroupedPayment::unallocatedAmount()` ([V] amount minus active allocations, `GroupedPayment.php:115`) stays positive and is **never** presented as spendable — a new `controlledAmount()` names it; `allocate()`'s availability check reads the same filtered source as its selection (gate suspected P2: filtering only `consumeEntries()` would overstate usable credit, `PaymentService.php:293-299`, `:585-592`); the per-customer `controlledReceipts()` read shows it with its reason and amount.
- **Exits of the overpayment receipt [P]:** **refund**, recorded by the consumer with means and confirmation date (the receipt is marked refunded; no fiscal document is produced by the package for the excess — whether any later fiscal treatment applies is **pending design**, by the operator's word); **reversal** of the whole payment (`reverse()`), which reverses the allocations as today and marks the receipt reversed — a state change, never a delete (G7, append-only). Never: the excess applied to another invoice, converted into credit afterwards, or added to a top-up.
- **Storage [P, one of two, §5.11]:** (a) a `controlled_receipts` table shared by every reason, which also gives the top-up receipts of §5.3 their home; or (b) nullable columns on `grouped_payments`. (a) is proposed because one list serves the four-plus-one reasons; it is a migration and is **not** decided.
- **Pending proof [T]:** two concurrent `receive()` with the same key and different policies conflict exactly once; a `reverse()` of an overpaid `Controlled` payment leaves no **active** controlled receipt and no entry — the receipt row stays, marked «reversed» with its history (G7, append-only); the upgrade path keeps every 9.1 payment at `Credit` with its entry untouched (no backfill, no reinterpretation).

### 5.3c Express application of a controlled receipt to a new accepted proforma (rev 6.9) — [B] D2 23:25, mechanism [P], proofs [T]

- **Business decision [B]:** the administration may apply a controlled receipt (reason `proforma_not_in_force`) expressly to a **new** proforma, once the customer accepted it: equal total → the receipt is applied; greater → the difference is charged; smaller → the difference is refunded. Never automatically. It is an express exception to the exact-amount rule. The technical mechanism is **not** decided by the operator; what follows is the proposal.
- **Operation [P]:** `applyControlledReceipt(ControlledReceipt $receipt, Invoice $newProforma, string $operationKey, string $actor)`, an act of the administration (a person, or a system the administration authorized through the API), never callable from the customer's flow and never triggered by the arrival of a payment. Preconditions, zero writes on failure: the receipt is controlled, unresolved, reason `proforma_not_in_force`, same customer and currency as the new proforma; the new proforma is a top-up proforma in force (DRAFT or SENT, never converted or cancelled); it resolves to **one** fiscal dimension (G6) — which need not equal the old proforma's: the new proforma carries the current conditions and prices (22:31). **Acceptance: the consumer's, not represented in the call** (14:55: the proof and the gate — the action exists only once the proof row for the new proforma exists — are clientes'; the package asserts and verifies nothing about it). The receipt's recorded payment is **never modified**.
- **Three cases, one rule — the sale starts only when the funded amount equals the new total (G5):**
  - **Equal:** the receipt funds the sale: S2 → S3a → S3b of §5.2 run for the new proforma with the receipt's payment as the sale's payment; S3b creates **one** entry (that payment) for the total; in the same write the receipt's resolution becomes «applied to <new proforma>».
  - **Greater:** the receipt is **earmarked** for the new proforma — a named, listed state «applied, pending difference» (`salesAwaitingDifference()`), before any conversion: nothing is converted, no balance exists. The customer pays the difference as a **separate payment with its own key**, through the ordinary channels; when a payment for exactly the difference is recorded against that proforma, the sale runs funded by both: S3b creates **two entries**, one per funding payment (the receipt's payment for the receipt amount, the difference payment for the difference), both backed by the same sale invoice and summing to its total (I4, rev 6.10 — gate V2: an entry carries a single payment, so two payments mean two entries, never one entry with two sources); reversal of either payment is refused once an entry backs an issued invoice (§5.2 step 6) and the refund path of §5.6 names the entry, hence the payment. **Conservation of amounts and VAT across the two payments (rev 6.11, round 3 V3) [P]:** [V] an exit's band is local to its entry's cent tape (`database/migrations/2026_10_26_000001_add_band_start_to_credit_movements.php:14-18`) while the pro-rata of a rectificative reads that band against the **whole** invoice's gross and tax (`src/Services/AdvanceInvoiceBuilder.php:359-389`), so two entries both starting at 0 would round the tax twice (100/17 split 50+50: 9 + 9 = 18). Each funding entry therefore receives an immutable **invoice-wide position** at S3b — `invoice_band_start`: the first entry [0, a), the second [a, total) — and every fiscal pro-rata on a voucher-backed exit uses `entry.invoice_band_start + exit.band_start` as its position on the **invoice** tape, with the last cent absorbed on the invoice tape, not per entry. Invariant: the sum of base and tax over all exits of both entries equals the invoice's base and tax exactly; gross equality alone is not conservation. Pending proof [T] on both engines. A payment for another amount is a controlled receipt as usual (`amount_mismatch`), never partial funding. If the difference never arrives, the administration **releases** the earmark by an express act (`releaseEarmark()`): the receipt returns to controlled, refundable; the new proforma may be cancelled by the consumer (§5.9).
  - **Smaller:** the receipt's payment funds the whole sale: S3b creates one entry for the new total; in the same write the excess becomes a **new controlled receipt** on that same payment, reason `application_excess`, subject = the new proforma, for refund by the same means. **Conservation (rev 6.10, gate V2):** for that payment, `amount = entry.amount + application_excess.amount`, and once the excess is refunded `reverse()` of the payment is refused (money already out; §7b S4). Created once: the excess receipt carries the application's operation key in its **creation key** `operation_key` (UNIQUE); the original receipt records the same application key in its **resolution key** `resolution_operation_key` (indexed, not unique — one receipt is resolved once on its own row). **Application-level identity (rev 6.16, gate round 6 B3):** the application itself is one row of `credit_operations` (kind `receipt_application`, `operation_key` UNIQUE, payload fingerprint = original receipt id + new proforma id + amounts), inserted in the application's transaction before any write; the same key used for another receipt or another proforma collides on that row and the call is refused as a conflict with nothing written, even though the receipts' and proformas' locks are disjoint. Two columns, because both rows carry the same application key (rev 6.15, gate round 5 V3: one UNIQUE column made the settled «smaller» case unrepresentable).
- **Idempotency [P]:** by `operationKey`: same key + same receipt + same new proforma → the recorded outcome (earmarked, sold, released); anything else → `IdempotencyConflictException::forKey()`. The difference payment's own key **never** re-applies the receipt; a replay of `sellVoucher()` with the original payment key still returns CONTROLLED. A crash after S3a leaves the same «numbered invoice without balance» state, listed and resumed by the application key.
- **Eligibility — restricted to the decided case ([B] D2 23:25, restricted 23:51):** a receipt is eligible for `applyControlledReceipt()` **only** when its **original reason** is `proforma_not_in_force` — money that arrived for a proforma already closed or not in force when it arrived — and it is unresolved. The original reason is **immutable** and eligibility is read from it, never derived from the proforma's later state: a receipt with reason `amount_mismatch` or `currency_mismatch` (received while the proforma was in force) is **not** eligible even if that proforma later expires or is cancelled — its exits stay refund (12:46) and reversal; a receipt with reason `proforma_not_convertible` is **not** eligible either — exceptional handling by the company (D2b), refund or reversal, no automatic resolution, even after the company cancels that proforma. **Traceability:** the receipt keeps original reason, subject proforma and recorded payment; the application records on it its resolution («applied», target = new proforma, actor, `resolution_operation_key`, instant); the old proforma keeps its own cancellation record (§5.9); nothing links the two proformas beyond that. **Case that would need a new decision of the operator, not asked here:** whether a refundable receipt of another reason could ever be applied expressly to a new proforma instead of being refunded and paid anew — under the current decisions it is refund only.
- **What stays the consumer's [B/C]:** when to offer the application (ticket, review), the acceptance of the new proforma and its proof, the charge of the difference and the refund of the excess (means, confirmation), the screens, the trace between the two proformas (the package stores the receipt's resolution target and nothing more).
- **Earmark protocol (rev 6.10, gate V6) [P]:** the earmark is written in **two places under locks** — on the receipt (resolution «earmarked», target = new proforma) and on the new proforma (`earmarked_receipt_id`) — in one transaction that takes the receipt's payment lock, then the proforma lock. While the proforma is earmarked: `sellVoucher()` on it with a fresh payment is refused unless that payment equals the **difference** (then it completes the sale with both); `cancelProforma()` refuses it (release first); a second `applyControlledReceipt()` targeting it, or using the same receipt, is refused; `convertProformaToInvoice()` called directly refuses it (its new under-lock status guard, §7b V5) — **except through the authorized completion (rev 6.11, round 3 V5):** the completion of an earmarked sale runs as `completeEarmarkedSale()`, which takes both funding-payment locks by id ascending → the proforma lock → re-checks under the locks that the earmark is intact and that the second payment equals the difference → then runs S2 with an internal, lock-scoped authorization token the public method validates (the earmark id it re-read under the same lock); any conversion of an earmarked proforma without that token is the bypass and stays refused. Lock order of the whole completion: payments asc → proforma → earmark re-check → conversion/invoice lock. `releaseEarmark()` clears both marks under the same two locks and returns the receipt to «controlled, unresolved»; **re-application after release** is allowed only with a **new** operation key (the old key replays «released»), and only while the receipt is unresolved. Lock order for the completion with two payments: the two payments by id ascending → proforma → invoice. Pending proof [T] below.
- **Pending proof [T]:** application vs `sellVoucher()` on the same new proforma (one wins under the proforma lock; a loser with an exact fresh payment is a DUPLICATE, §5.2b); application vs `cancelProforma()`; two applications with the same key; the difference payment arriving twice (second is a DUPLICATE); release vs difference arrival (one wins; never a released earmark with a sale); the excess receipt created exactly once (UNIQUE on the application key); two entries summing exactly to the invoice total on both engines.

### 5.4 Consumption exit

```php
public function consumeVoucher(
    string $billableUserId,
    FixedDecimal $amount,            // gross, in the voucher's dimension, > 0
    string $currency,
    VoucherConsumption $what,        // service reference, description, consumed_at, actor, declared tax group / treatment
    string $operationKey,
): Collection                        // the CONSUMED exits of the operation, tape order (one per band gap)
```

1. Outside the transaction: validate amount and key; select the customer's active voucher-backed entries with a positive remainder (FIFO by `occurred_at`, `id`), read their payment ids.
2. Transaction; lock those payments by id ascending (the existing source-payment lock of exits).
3. Adoption by key after the locks (same payload → return the recorded exits; different → conflict). **Durable identity (rev 6.15, gate round 5 V4):** the operation is one row of `credit_operations` (uuid id, `operation_key` UNIQUE, kind consumption | refund, `billable_user_id`, payload fingerprint, `recorded_at`), inserted in the same transaction before the exits; every exit it writes carries `credit_operation_id`. One operation may write several exits (one per band gap, §5.4 step 5), so the exits themselves carry no UNIQUE key. Two same-key calls that lock disjoint payments do not serialize on the locks; the loser's insert of the operation row fails (SQLSTATE 23000), its transaction rolls back with nothing written, and it re-reads the committed operation outside the transaction: same fingerprint → returns the recorded exits; other → conflict.
4. **Refusals, zero writes:** no voucher-backed balance; remaining < amount (G3, N2 — the whole amount or nothing; what the business does next — require payment or top-up first, or provide or renew first and collect later — is its policy, D3 / C10; after a new top-up the consumer simply calls again); [P, pending H3] the declared dimension differs from the frozen one of the entry that would be consumed (G12); the consumption would span entries of different dimensions (it must then be refused as a whole, not split).
5. Plan FIFO over voucher-backed entries only; band placement unchanged (lowest free gap under the lock, one exit row per gap); exit kind **CONSUMED**: no `allocation_id`, no deduction line, carries service reference, description, consumed_at, actor, operation key.
6. **Reversal of a consumption** (an error corrected by the consumer): the exit is reversed by `reversed_at` under the same lock, restoring the remainder; the consumer's receipt for it is the consumer's to void.

The scope check (N4: is this service covered by the accepted conditions?) is the consumer's, before the call (C1). The package has no catalogue of covered services; whether it stores an opaque scope reference on the entry for the consumer's convenience is a technical choice (§12).

### 5.5 Consumption receipt data (option a)

The package does not add a document type in v1 (D7 stays). `consumeVoucher()` returns, per exit, and a read `consumptionReceiptData(exitId)` re-reads: sale invoice number and date, consumed gross amount, the dimension frozen on the entry (rate or treatment, informative), remaining balance of the entry and of the customer's voucher balance after the exit, exit id, service reference, description, consumed_at. The consumer renders the receipt in its own non-fiscal series with the mention «no es una factura» (C7). The receipt never enters a fiscal series nor VeriFactu. **[F H1]** whether this receipt satisfies every documentary obligation is not asserted; if the check fails, the fallback is a consumption **invoice** with a deduction of the voucher — AID-1815's primitive, kept for that reason (§11). Option (b) — a package model and template under ADR-011 — is listed in §12 for when a second consumer needs it.

### 5.6 Refund of voucher balance (capability; policy the consumer's)

- **Mechanism (rev 5 §6.5, kept):** `refundEntries()` over named entries with an operation key, adoption after the locks, FIFO over the named set, one amount; the UNIQUE backstop is the operation row of §5.4 step 3 (`credit_operations`, kind refund), not a column on the exits (rev 6.15, gate round 5 V4) [T, gate S1]. Voucher-backed entries are refundable **only** through this targeted call (G2 keeps them out of the untargeted `refund()`).
- **Fiscal document:** for the refunded part of a voucher-backed entry the package offers the partial rectificative of the sale invoice, computed pro-rata on the exit's stored band against the sale invoice's frozen totals (the arithmetic of `rectificativeLines()`, `src/Services/AdvanceInvoiceBuilder.php:269`, generalised from "advance" to "backing invoice"; the cent is absorbed by the **invoice-wide** position of §5.3c — the exit whose band ends at the invoice total — not by the last exit of each entry, so two funding entries never round twice; rev 6.15 corrects the earlier «last exit of an entry», gate round 5 V6). The rectificative goes through `SettlementService::rectify()` unchanged.
- **What the package does not decide [B]:** whether a refund is allowed (clientes v1: no ordinary refund), under which legal obligations it must be honoured, and **which document corresponds in each case** [F H4] — the package exposes the rectificative path and records the exit; the consumer calls it when its policy or the law requires it. The H3 settlement path (settle the unconsumed part before a fiscal change takes effect) is this capability applied before an effective date; which changes require it is [F H3].

### 5.7 Advance path (AID-1815 territory, except the whole-entry rule of rev 5 D7, enforced in this release — rev 6.19)

### 5.9 Proforma expiry; a closed proforma stays closed (rev 6.5, decision 22:31)

- **Expiry (C6, [B] 14:43):** the consumer decides the validity (it may use `due_date`) and cancels the proforma as expired with a reason; the package adds no timer. A cancelled proforma receives no sale (precondition of §5.2).
- **No reopening, no reactivation ([B] 22:31):** «una proforma cerrada no se reabre ni se reactiva, aunque el precio no haya cambiado». The package offers **no** transition from CANCELLED back to DRAFT and this proposal adds none ([V] none exists today, `src/Enums/InvoiceStatus.php:20-28`); the `reactivateProforma()` capability of rev 6.1–6.4, its authorization rules and the open question on states, reasons and audit are **withdrawn**. The previous proforma stays closed with its history, which is never cleared.
- **Erratum of rev 6.1 gap 3 (corrected in rev 6.8, D12):** a proforma has **no document axis** — `invoice_document_status` is NULL on every PROFORMA row and the model forbids any `document_cancelled_at/by` on it (`src/Models/Invoice.php:405-456`, rules C2 and S5). The «two axes» described a fiscal draft cancelled directly, not a proforma. A cancelled proforma today is `status = CANCELLED` and nothing else: no instant, no actor, no reason. That is the gap the next bullet fills.
- **`cancelProforma()` — safe expiry cancellation (rev 6.8, G13) [P]:**

  ```php
  public function cancelProforma(Invoice $proforma, string $actor, ProformaCancellationReason $reason, ?string $note = null): Invoice
  ```

  Under the proforma row lock (`lockForUpdate`, the same first lock `convertProformaToInvoice()` takes, so a cancellation and a conversion of the same proforma serialize): requires serie PROFORMA; `status` DRAFT or SENT; `converted_invoice_id` NULL and no PREPARED child (a proforma mid-conversion is CONVERTING and is refused — the consumer first abandons the sale with `cancelPreparedInvoice()`, which returns it to DRAFT); then writes `status = CANCELLED` and the **cancellation record** (instant from the database clock, actor, reason — `expired`, `customer_request`, `staff`, `superseded` —, optional note). Refusals, zero writes: CONVERTING / CONVERTED / already CANCELLED (`InvoiceNotCancellableException`, new named constructors); not a proforma (the fiscal paths are `cancelPreparedInvoice()` for a PREPARED document and `SettlementService::annul()` for an issued one — this method never touches them). It never changes `is_immutable`, never writes the document axis (S5 holds), never numbers anything. A second call on a CANCELLED proforma is a refusal, not a no-op, so the consumer's sweep reads before it writes.
- **Where the cancellation record lives [P, one of two, §5.11]:** (a) nullable columns on `invoices` for the **collection axis**, valid on proforma rows, with a new model rule that is **one-directional**, so it is compatible with proformas already cancelled in 9.1 and with the no-backfill decision: «any of `cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by`, `cancellation_reason`, `cancellation_note` NOT NULL ⇒ all of `cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by`, `cancellation_reason` NOT NULL **and** `status = CANCELLED` **and** serie PROFORMA» — the record is all-or-nothing (rev 6.10, gate V9: an implication on `cancelled_at` alone let `cancelled_by` sit on a live proforma); a record never sits on a live proforma nor on a fiscal row; a fiscal row annulled by `annul()` also has `status = CANCELLED`, `src/Services/SettlementService.php:218`, and keeps its document-axis audit). The converse is **not** required: a CANCELLED proforma with `cancelled_at` NULL is a valid row and means «cancelled without a record» — every proforma cancelled before this release (9.1 data, no backfill) and any later direct `status = CANCELLED` write by a consumer. **How the two shapes are distinguished:** the read `cancellationRecord()` returns the record (instant, actor, reason, note) or `null`; the consumer shows «sin registro de anulación» for `null`; nothing infers a reason for it. The sweep and the sale primitive treat both shapes the same way (not in force); or (b) a `proforma_cancellations` table. (a) is proposed; both are a migration. The consumer-side sweep of clientes (rev16 §5.6) calls this method and adds nothing of its own.
- **What stays the consumer's [B]:** when a proforma expires (`due_date`, C6), who may cancel, and what the customer sees. The package only guarantees that the cancellation is one coherent write with its audit.
- **Continuation ([B] 22:31, consumer side):** the customer asks by ticket; the administration reviews the request and, if it proceeds, issues a **new** proforma with the current conditions for the customer's acceptance — a new step 1 and step 2 of §2, a new acceptance proof (14:55), a new sale when the exact amount is received. The package sees an ordinary new proforma; nothing links it to the closed one unless the consumer records that link on its side.
- **A payment received for the closed proforma ([B] 22:31, 22:40):** it is recorded and controlled (reason `proforma_not_in_force`); it reopens nothing and pays nothing; no link to the new proforma is invented. Its resolution is a separate case the normal flow does not depend on: refund, or the express application of §5.3c (D2, 23:25).
- **Expiry is not annulment (rev 6.1, gap 3):** a cancelled proforma carries its proforma number (proformas are numbered at birth in their non-fiscal series — model rule C3, `src/Models/Invoice.php:410-413`; `createProforma()`, `src/Services/InvoiceService.php:700-705`) but **no fiscal number**: nothing fiscal was emitted, nothing is annulled (rev 6.11 corrects rev 6.1's «never-numbered»). An **issued** invoice — the voucher-sale invoice included — is never cancelled or expired; it is annulled only through `SettlementService::annul()` (`RegistroAnulacion`, ADR-014) or corrected through a rectificative.

### 5.10 Fiscal submission and consumption — decided 22:22 (pending) and 22:25 (confirmed rejection)

- [V, corrected rev 6.10] `issue()` dispatches nothing; the fiscal verification is initiated by the calling wrapper when `verify_fiscally` is requested (`src/Services/InvoiceService.php:285`, `:543`, `:999` → `verifyInvoiceFiscally()` `:1069`); the sale primitive initiates nothing — the consumer registers the issued sale invoice after SOLD (§5.2, rev 6.20). The result (acceptance, rejection, retry) arrives later and asynchronously, in the external package. **VeriFactu is not assumed active in every installation:** an installation without it has no such result at all.
- The balance of §5.2 S3b exists as soon as the sale invoice is numbered and the entry is created, before any result of the submission.
- **Decision [B] (22:22):** once the payment is confirmed and the invoice issued, the balance is available for consumption even while the fiscal submission is pending of dispatch or of answer; the incident or the wait is handled separately and does not block the customer. Package behaviour **for the pending case only:** `consumeVoucher()` does not wait for the dispatch or the answer of the submission; it is allowed from S3b while the submission is pending. Consumption after a **confirmed rejection** is decided and allowed under the conditions of the next bullet (22:25); `consumeVoucher()` is not blocked by the submission state in either case, and the submission state stays readable from the fiscal package for visibility, not for blocking. Incidents of the submission belong to the consumer and to the fiscal package, outside the ledger.
- **Decision [B] (22:25) — confirmed rejection:** with the payment confirmed, the invoice issued and the balance in existence, the customer keeps consuming it even if the tax agency rejected the invoicing record. The rejection must stay visible and be handled through the applicable correction procedure («subsanación»); it is neither ignored nor treated as accepted. Package behaviour: `consumeVoucher()` is not blocked by the submission state in either case; **visibility** is the consumer's and the fiscal package's — the submission state lives in the external package today, and this revision adds no copy of it to the ledger; the consumer's screens read it from there. **Not covered by this decision [B]:** an invoice issued in error, or an operation that must be corrected — those keep their own paths (annulment through `SettlementService::annul()`, rectificative), and what they mean for the voucher balance is not decided here. **VeriFactu is not assumed active in every installation:** an installation without a fiscal package has no submission and no rejection.

### 5.13 Annulment guard: no annulled invoice may carry live linked balance (rev 6.12, completed in rev 6.14) — [B] 08:42/09:32, mechanism [P], proofs [T]

**The gap [V].** `assertAnnullable()` refuses a proforma, a collection DRAFT, a CANCELLED invoice, an invoice with rectificatives and one with active allocations (`src/Services/SettlementService.php:470-493`). It does not look at `credit_movements`, where an advance's (and, under §5.1, a voucher sale's) money lives. Any linked fiscal invoice at another collection status can therefore be annulled today while its entry still holds balance: the VAT document is withdrawn (`RegistroAnulacion`) and the balance stays spendable as backed money pointing at an annulled invoice.

**The invariant, stated once [P] — limited to what the package can protect (rev 6.15, gate round 5 V1):** **no write made through the package after this release leaves an invoice annulled while an unreversed entry linked to it — by `advance_invoice_id` or `sale_invoice_id` — has a remaining above zero.** It does **not** cover (i) states already present in a 9.1 database — 9.1 allows linking a live entry, issuing, a consumer stamp SENT and `annul()`; those rows are listed by the diagnosis (§5.12.2) and never modified; (ii) direct writes by a consumer to `invoices.status`, `advance_invoice_id`, `sale_invoice_id` or `reversed_at` that bypass the package's methods; (iii) **the deprecated public method `Invoice::restoreStateViaGroupedPaymentReversal(InvoiceStatus $status, ?DateTimeInterface $paidAt)`** (`src/Models/Invoice.php:735-745`, `@deprecated since 8.3.0`, removal in 10.0, still in the contract snapshot `tests/Contract/snapshots/Invoice.json`) — named, not folded into (ii) (rev 6.16, gate round 6 B2).

**What (iii) can break, and why it is not a direct database write [V]:** it assigns any `InvoiceStatus`, including CANCELLED, and calls `save()` with no linked-balance check, so a caller can leave a live-linked invoice, or an issued sale not yet backed, at CANCELLED without going through `annul()` — and therefore without the `RegistroAnulacion` path and without guards 1–3. Unlike a direct database write it is package code: it runs inside the model, fires Eloquent events and the model's state checks (`Invoice::checkStateViolations()`), is part of the `@api` surface covered by `STABILITY.md` and its docblock already states that «a consumer calling this overwrites the derived state under its own responsibility». **Not guarded in this release:** adding a refusal to a deprecated method would narrow surface that rule 3 says keeps working until removed (`STABILITY.md:26-30`); the CHANGELOG states that the §5.13 guarantee does not hold for invoices whose status was written through it, and its docblock says the same. `markAsPaidViaGroupedPayment()` (`Invoice.php:728-733`) writes only PAID and cannot produce the forbidden state.

**The `rectify()` / `annul()` race (gate round 6 B4) — examined, separate [V]:** `SettlementService::rectify()` checks `assertRectifiable()` on the instance the caller passed and creates the rectificative through `createInvoice()` without locking the original (`src/Services/SettlementService.php:121-160`); `annul()` locks the invoice but counts rectificatives and active allocations with plain reads (`:209-221, 470-493`). A rectificative and an annulment of the same invoice can therefore both commit — in 9.1 today, with or without a caller transaction. **Effect on this release's guarantee:** none on the invariant of §5.13 — a rectificative does not change any entry's remaining or link (`rectify()` writes no `credit_movements` row), so the race cannot leave live linked balance on an annulled invoice. **Effect on the new flow:** the voucher refund (§5.6) ends in a partial rectificative of the sale invoice through `rectify()` unchanged, so it **inherits** the race: an `annul()` of the sale invoice — allowed once its remaining is zero — racing that rectificative can leave an annulled invoice with an issued rectificative. That is the same fiscal inconsistency 9.1 already allows for every invoice, not a new one. **Separated as AID-1833** (larabill, related to AID-1825; created 2026-11-01 under the operator's 10:45 authorization; separating it does not accept the risk); an explicit limitation in the CHANGELOG and on both methods; this release does not change `rectify()`. **Does the voucher refund depend on resolving it? No — on bounding it, and guard 1(c) bounds it (rev 6.17):** once a refund exit exists on a voucher-sale invoice's entry, `annul()` of that invoice is refused, so the partial rectificative of §5.6 can never race a successful annulment of it; before any exit, the entry's remaining is positive and guard 1(a) refuses. The bound holds only if guards 1(a) and 1(c) read entries and exits as specified (locking reads under the invoice lock, «Common protocol» below). It does not cover other invoices, nor a voucher-sale invoice whose entries are all reversed (no active entry: neither guard applies — then `annul()` behaves as for any invoice and AID-1833's race applies). Publishing the voucher refund relies on this bound, not on AID-1833. Gate round 4 showed that a check inside `annul()` alone does not keep it (V2, V3): the remaining can be restored after an annulment, and a sale's entry can be created after one. The invariant therefore has three guards, one per way in.

**Guard 1 — `annul()` [P].** `assertAnnullable()` gains two conditions, after the existing ones:

- (a) refuse when any unreversed entry linked to the invoice (either link) has a remaining above zero — `InvoiceNotAnnullableException::hasLiveLinkedBalance($invoice, $entryIds)`;
- (b) refuse when the invoice carries a sale claim (§5.2, `sale_claim_id`) whose stage is not «backed» — `InvoiceNotAnnullableException::unbackedSale($invoice)`; an issued sale without its entry is abandoned only through `abandonUnbackedSale()` (§5.2), whatever collection status a consumer stamped on it (gate round 4, V3: S3a → consumer SENT → `annul()` was allowed). **Scope (rev 6.15, gate round 5 V5):** guard 1(b) applies to the public `annul()` only; `abandonUnbackedSale()` issues its annulment through the shared internal annulment step **after** its own preconditions (stage «issuing», no entry, sale marker present, under its locks) and does not pass through guard 1(b) — exactly as it already bypasses the DRAFT refusal by its marker-scoped exception. Guard 1(a) still applies to it and is trivially met (no entry exists).

- (c) **voucher-sale invoices only (rev 6.17, operator decision 2026-11-01 10:45, gate round 7 V3):** refuse when any **unreversed** entry linked to the invoice by `sale_invoice_id` has at least one **active** exit, of any kind (CONSUMED, REFUNDED, APPLIED), whatever its remaining — `InvoiceNotAnnullableException::hasUsedVoucherBalance($invoice, $exitIds)`. Definitions: an exit is **active** while its `reversed_at` is NULL; a **reversed** exit (consumption reversal, §5.4 step 6; a credit-funded allocation reversed, W4) does not count — its amount is back in the entry's remaining, which guard 1(a) then sees; a **reversed entry** (its payment reversed, W3) counts for neither guard. Why: without 1(c) a refund taking the remaining to zero made the sale invoice annullable, and the rectificative the refund needs (§5.6) would then be refused by `assertRectifiable()` (`SettlementService.php:500-515`) — a refund recorded without its fiscal document, sequentially, with no concurrency. **Not decided here:** how a voucher-sale invoice issued in error that already has balance movements is corrected; that exceptional procedure is outside this decision, and neither guard 1(c) nor this spec states that such an invoice is always corrected one single way. Advance links (`advance_invoice_id`) keep guard 1(a) only; whether an advance whose balance was used may be annulled is not decided by this revision. **Evaluation order (rev 6.19, gate round 8 B5):** after the existing refusals, 1(b), then **1(c), then 1(a)** — a voucher-sale invoice with any active exit reports `hasUsedVoucherBalance` whatever its remaining, and 1(a) reports every other live case. Both refuse; the order fixes which cause is reported and makes each guard's mutation observable in §10.

Both are new named constructors on the existing `@api` exception, in its existing style (`src/Exceptions/InvoiceNotAnnullableException.php:24-65`); zero writes on refusal.

**Guard 2 — every write that restores a linked entry's remaining [P, rev 6.16 option A, pending proof].** Today that is `PaymentService::reverseAllocation()` when it reverses credit-funded APPLIED exits (`src/Services/PaymentService.php:420-459`); in this design also the reversal of a consumption (§5.4 step 6). Such a path refuses — `CreditEntryBackingAnnulledException` (new, `@api`), zero writes — when the entry whose remaining it restores is linked to an annulled invoice. **Gate round 6 B1 [V]:** rev 6.15 read the links before the transaction and re-read them, but `linkAdvanceInvoice()` locks only the invoice and the entry it updates — never the payment — and does not look at exits (`src/Models/CreditMovement.php:231-281`), so a link could be written after the re-read and before the exits were reversed.

Lock order (option A):

1. the payment(s), as today (`PaymentService.php:428`);
2. the invoices it already knows — the allocation's target and the backing invoices found by a plain read before the transaction — in one ascending-id lock (`Ledger::lockInvoices()`, `src/Services/Payments/Ledger.php:36-55`);
3. **the entry rows whose remaining it restores**, by locking read on their primary keys, ascending;
4. the links read **from those locked rows**: every `advance_invoice_id` / `sale_invoice_id` must be among the invoices locked in step 2; if one is not (a link committed between the plain read and step 3), the call **aborts with nothing written** — `CreditEntryLinkChangedException` (new, `@api`, retryable); if a locked backing invoice is CANCELLED, it refuses with `CreditEntryBackingAnnulledException`;
5. the exit writes.

Why step 3 closes B1: `linkAdvanceInvoice()` must update the entry row, so while the reversal holds it the link waits; the link, the issue and the annulment of a new advance can therefore no longer happen between the reversal's check and its write. A link that committed before step 3 is seen in step 4 (a locking read returns the latest committed row — engine behaviour, proven by the test below, not measured here). **Since rev 6.19** this window is closed before step 3 for every link made through the package: the link takes the entry's payment first, which the reversal holds from its first statement, and it refuses an entry with active exits (rev 5 D7), which is exactly what a reversal restores; step 4 remains the defence for inherited rows and for writes outside the package (test (c) below).

**Deadlock analysis (on paper, not proven; rewritten in rev 6.19 for rule 1 revised):** `linkAdvanceInvoice()` takes payment P → advance invoice X → entry E (record locks only). The reversal takes P → {known invoices} → E. Both take P first, so they serialize on P and cannot hold each other's next resource. The allocation's target cannot be X: an advance being linked is an unnumbered DRAFT and is not payable (`Ledger::payableStatuses()`, `Ledger.php:144`). FK shared locks: the link's UPDATE of E takes a shared lock on X, which it already holds exclusively; the reversal's exit UPDATEs take shared locks on E, which it already holds exclusively. ### 5.15 Targeted advance deduction on a draft (rev 6.20, operator decision 2026-11-01 13:10) — [B], mechanism [P], proofs [T]

**Decision [B] (13:10):** «esta versión debe conservar un camino para consumir saldo respaldado por un anticipo y obtener su línea de deducción. No retiréis esa capacidad hasta AID-1815 ni reutilicéis sin protección una aplicación de saldo que pueda duplicar el IVA.»

**What 9.1 offers, measured [V] (gate round 9 B2):** `AdvanceInvoiceBuilder::deductionLine()` (`@api`, AID-1778) builds the frozen negative line of an **active APPLIED exit** whose entry is linked to the advance (`src/Services/AdvanceInvoiceBuilder.php:210-236, 313-333`); the only package writers of APPLIED exits are `CustomerCredit::apply()` and `PaymentService::consumeEntries()` (`CustomerCredit.php:285`, `PaymentService.php:562, 576`), both as **payment of an invoice** through an allocation. G2 (§4, §5.1 I2) removes advance-backed entries from both, so without a new path this release would leave `deductionLine()` with no package-made exit to read. And 9.1 itself ties nothing together: `apply()` needs a payable target (`Ledger::payableStatuses()`, `Ledger.php:144` — never a DRAFT), while the deduction line must enter the later invoice's items before it is issued; a consumer that applies advance balance as payment of an issued invoice without a deduction line declares the VAT twice (§8 row 1). AID-1778's sandbox case built its exit by hand (`docs/specs/aid-1778-advance-invoice-partial-card.md:233`; `clientes` `tests/Feature/Integration/Aid1778AdvanceSandboxTest.php:171-181`).

**Proposal [P] — one targeted, keyed operation that writes the exit and its deduction line together, never a payment:**

```php
// @api — names are the builder's; the behaviour is the contract
public function deductAdvanceOnDraft(
    string $operationKey,           // idempotency key (credit_operations, kind advance_deduction)
    Invoice $draft,                 // the LATER invoice: fiscal INVOICE, document DRAFT, collection DRAFT, unnumbered
    string $entryId,                // an advance-backed entry (advance_invoice_id set)
    FixedDecimal $amount,           // > 0, ≤ the entry's remaining
): AdvanceDeduction                 // the exit(s), the line(s) written on the draft, the operation row
```

1. **Outermost transaction required** (`OutermostTransaction::assert()`, as `apply()` and `reverseAllocation()`, `CustomerCredit.php:246`, `PaymentService.php:422`) — a new method, so no existing call shape is narrowed. Discovery outside the transaction by plain read: the entry's payment and advance.
2. **Locks:** the entry's payment P → invoices ascending {draft, advance} (`Ledger::lockInvoices()`) → the entry row by primary key (`FOR UPDATE`) → its links read from the locked row: `advance_invoice_id` must equal the locked advance (else `CreditEntryLinkChangedException`, nothing written).
3. **Checks on the locked rows, zero writes on refusal:** the draft is a fiscal INVOICE, document DRAFT, collection DRAFT, unnumbered, of the entry's `billable_user_id` and currency, is not the advance itself, is not linked as an advance by any entry, and carries no sale claim; the entry is an entry, unreversed, advance-backed, with `remaining ≥ amount`; the advance is document ISSUED and collection not CANCELLED; the operation key is new (same key + same payload → the recorded outcome; other payload → `IdempotencyConflictException::forKey()`).
4. **Writes, one transaction:** the operation row (`credit_operations`, new kind `advance_deduction`, UNIQUE `operation_key`); one APPLIED exit per free band gap of the entry (the existing band assignment under the payment lock, lowest free gap first), with `allocation_id` NULL and the new column `deduction_invoice_id` = the draft; for each exit, the line `deductionLine($exit)` returns, inserted into the draft's items on the frozen path (`deducts_invoice_id` = the advance); the draft's totals recomputed. **Why this cannot declare the VAT twice:** no allocation is written and no invoice is paid; the money leaves the entry only as a negative line on the draft, priced from the advance's frozen VAT, and the exit and its line exist together or not at all.
5. **Before the draft is issued — undo:** `reverseAdvanceDeduction(string $operationKey)` (`@api`), same locks, draft still DRAFT: removes the operation's lines from the draft, stamps its exits `reversed_at`, recomputes the draft. It restores a remaining, so **guard 2** applies: advance collection CANCELLED → `CreditEntryBackingAnnulledException`, zero writes (§5.13).
6. **At issue — the draft carries exactly its deductions:** `issue()` of a draft that has at least one active exit with `deduction_invoice_id` = that draft refuses, under its invoice lock, when (i) an active deduction exit has no matching line (same advance, same frozen amounts), (ii) a line with `deducts_invoice_id` has no matching active exit, or (iii) a deducted advance is collection CANCELLED — new `InvalidAdvanceDeductionException` (`@api`), zero writes. A draft with no deduction exit — including drafts a 9.1 consumer composed with hand-built deduction lines — issues exactly as in 9.1.
7. **Deleting the draft:** the new FK `deduction_invoice_id` is `restrictOnDelete()`, so a draft that carries deduction exits — active or reversed, since a reversed exit keeps its `deduction_invoice_id` for traceability — cannot be deleted, through the model, the query builder or SQL. Consequence, stated: such a draft is kept or issued, never deleted. It is a new column on a new state, so no deletion that works on 9.1 changes; the advance FK of §5.13 is untouched (operator 12:36).
8. **After issue:** the deduction is final; its correction is a rectificative of the later invoice under ADR-014 — **not** in this delivery (AID-1815).

**Lock order and cycles (on paper, not proven):** P → {draft, advance} ascending → entry. `apply()`/`allocate()`/`refund()`/`reverse()`/`reverseAllocation()`/consumption/`refundEntries()` and `linkAdvanceInvoice()` take P first (§5.13). `issue(draft)` takes the draft only (`InvoiceService.php:331-335`); `annul(advance)` takes the advance, then its link set (locking reads). Neither holds P, so each serializes with this operation on the invoice row it shares. Exercised in §9 case 17.

**What belongs to this delivery and what stays in AID-1815:**

| This delivery (AID-1825) | AID-1815 |
|---|---|
| `deductAdvanceOnDraft()` and `reverseAdvanceDeduction()` over **advance-backed** entries on an **unissued draft**, keyed, traceable (`deduction_invoice_id`, `credit_operations`), with guard 2 on the undo and the issue-time check | deduction over **voucher-backed** entries if H1 fails (§11 (i)); correction of a deduction after the later invoice is issued (rectificative of the later invoice); the payment-on-account flows of §11 (ii); any consumer screen or policy |

**Observable changes against 9.1 (§8 rows 1, 16, 17):** consuming advance-backed balance moves from `apply()`/`allocate()` (removed by G2, row 1) to the targeted operation (row 16, additive); `issue()` refuses a draft whose deduction exits and lines do not match (row 17 — reachable only on drafts that received a deduction through the new operation, so no 9.1 draft is affected). **Schema (`'055'`):** `credit_movements.deduction_invoice_id` nullable FK → `invoices`, `restrictOnDelete()`, indexed, **not fillable**; `credit_operations.kind` gains `advance_deduction`.

**Readers of APPLIED exits [V], checked so the allocation-less exit does not change them:** `PaymentService::reverseAllocation()` selects by `allocation_id` (`:436-442`); `PaymentService::directAllocationMap()` joins by `allocation_id` (`:625-640`); `DomPDFService` reads credit-funded allocations through `creditMovements` of the allocation (`src/Services/PDF/DomPDFService.php:533-545`). An exit with `allocation_id` NULL is invisible to the three; `remaining()` counts it (`CreditMovement.php:143-154`), which is what consumes the balance. Any other reader the census search misses is a census gap (§5.14).

**Tests (pending, three CI combinations, each asserting by name that it ran) [T]:** deduction of part of an entry → one exit, one line on the draft with the advance's frozen base and VAT, remaining reduced, no allocation; a deduction spanning two free gaps → two exits, two lines, sums exact (telescoping of AID-1778); same key same payload → replay, other payload → conflict; draft not DRAFT, other customer, other currency, the advance itself, an unbacked or voucher-backed entry, amount above remaining, advance annulled → refused with zero writes; `reverseAdvanceDeduction()` → lines removed, exits reversed, remaining restored; with the advance annulled meanwhile → `CreditEntryBackingAnnulledException`; `issue()` with a line removed by hand, a line added by hand, or a deducted advance annulled → `InvalidAdvanceDeductionException`; a 9.1-style draft with a hand-built deduction line and no deduction exit → issues as in 9.1; deleting a draft with a deduction exit → refused by the FK on both engines; `apply()` on the same customer never selects the advance entry (G2). Mutations that must fail: writing the exit without the line (or the line without the exit); dropping the issue-time check (i); allowing a payable target. Concurrency: §9 case 17.
