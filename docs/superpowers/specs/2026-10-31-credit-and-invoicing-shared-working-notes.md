## 2. Boundary to establish

Larabill records fiscal documents, payments and credit, preserving their relationships and enforcing package invariants. A consuming application supplies business context, permissions and workflow decisions. This is a starting proposal: the exact responsibility for each behavior must be decided, not inferred from where a field currently lives.

In particular, receipt of money, allocation to an invoice, the payer's intent, performance of the underlying operation, and the fiscal document are distinct facts. A proforma is not itself evidence that a payment is an advance, an error, or taxable; fiscal consequences depend on the facts and applicable rules. Labels and configuration cannot make an otherwise unlawful transaction compliant.

### 3.1 Business clarification on top-ups (operator, 2026-10-31) — for joint review, not an approval of rev 5

- **A contracted top-up is distinct from money received in error.** The two stay separate cases (the error case is the row «Proforma 100, 20 received in error» and the row «Receipt with purpose not yet established»); they must never be modelled as one.
- **The main flow of clientes:** top up → document fiscally → consume. Top up and refund is not the main flow.
- **One fiscal dimension:** the balance and its consumptions are expressed in the same fiscal dimension — with the taxes applicable to the top-up, or without them when none apply. Each consumption reduces the balance in that dimension.
- **Left open — base and tax:** how the base and the taxes are preserved and applied from the top-up to each consumption, especially when the fiscal context changes between the two. Nothing here asserts that the legal treatment of that case is resolved.
- **Refund policy varies by consumer or company.** Not offering the ordinary refund of contracted balance is the policy clientes proposes; it is **not** a universal prohibition of the package, and another consumer may allow such refunds. The **policy** of authorizing a refund (a consumer or company decision) is kept apart from the **capability** of the invoicing engine to record a refund that was decided and to issue the fiscal document that corresponds to it (a package matter).
- **Left open — refunds:** the scope and the location of any configuration for that policy (installation, legal issuer, customer, transaction — see §4); the legal obligations under which a refund must be honoured regardless of the contractual policy; and the cases in which a refund requires a rectificative of the advance document. The corresponding fiscal document is pending analysis.
- **Operator decision — the top-up is requested and accepted before payment (2026-10-31):** in the clientes top-up flow, the customer expressly requests the top-up and accepts its conditions **before** paying. A receipt of money, on its own, proves neither that request nor that acceptance. Left open for the joint review: how the proof of acceptance is recorded, and what happens when the amount received differs from the amount requested.
- **Operator decision — exact amount only (2026-10-31, clientes policy):** a top-up is accepted only for the exact amount that was requested and accepted. When a different amount is received, its refund is processed: it is not turned into a partial top-up or a top-up for another amount, and any future exception to this requires an express decision. The money received is recorded and kept under control until it is returned; the return goes through the same means it came in — a transfer by transfer, a card payment by card — and is not considered made until it is confirmed. Left open: how the differences and the operational exceptions are handled technically. This is a clientes policy, **not** a universal prohibition of Larabill.
- **Operator decision — mismatched receipt leaves the original proforma in force (2026-10-31, clientes policy; relayed by the clientes session, owner wording verified):** when the amount received differs from the one requested for a top-up, that receipt is refunded (previous decision) and the **original** top-up proforma remains in force after that incorrect receipt — until the customer cancels it, until it reaches its expiry date, or until it is resolved through the applicable top-up flow. For a top-up, expiry means **only** annulling the proforma — nothing else happens. A configurable expiry period may be deferred to a later version if it is an obstacle now. Explicitly **not** decided yet: the technical mechanism, the closing state on fulfilment, and the treatment of a payment that arrives after expiry.
- **Operator decision — correct payment received after the expiry of a top-up proforma (2026-10-31, clientes policy):** expiry, on its own, does not oblige to refund that payment. The **same** proforma can be reactivated to continue the top-up flow. The reactivation requires an express action by a person or by a system through the API; it is **not** automatic upon the arrival of the payment. Still to be defined: the mechanism, the conditions and the record of that reactivation. Do not assume automatic bank access.
- **Operator decision — proof of acceptance belongs to the consumer (2026-10-31, clientes policy; relayed by the clientes session, owner wording verified):** clientes is responsible for collecting and retaining the proof that the customer accepted the top-up conditions **before** paying. clientes defines the method and the data to keep — including IP address, date and time — and how the accepted conditions are identified. Larabill must **not** impose the capture mechanism nor the storage of that proof. Left pending: the concrete design of that proof and its privacy review. This closes the "how the proof of acceptance is recorded" point left open in the decision above on request and acceptance before payment.
- **Operator decision — exact amount received on a top-up proforma converts it into a fiscal invoice (2026-10-31, clientes policy):** when the exact amount of a top-up proforma that is in force — or expressly reactivated — is received and correctly recorded, the flow must convert that proforma into a numbered and sealed fiscal invoice and generate the balance in favour of the customer. The later consumption or exhaustion of that balance does not determine the closure of the proforma. The payment may come from a bank, PayPal or another means; no automatic bank detection is assumed. Left pending: checking whether the current conversion of Larabill supports this outcome, and how to guarantee the flow in the face of failures. This decision **replaces** the proposal of a "fulfilled" state for this case (rev 5 proposal D6, option A, and the related open item in §5).

## 4. Consumer-specific configuration — open design question

Different consuming applications may choose different workflows. Configuration is a candidate mechanism, **not an approved design**. Before selecting one, decide:

1. **Scope:** Is a setting per installation/application, legal issuer, customer, transaction, or some combination? The term “user” is ambiguous; do not assume it means the billed customer.
2. **Authority:** Who selects a workflow and who can change it? What happens to transactions already in progress when a setting changes?
3. **Choices:** May a consumer reject initiating a partial proforma payment, accept and hold an incoming transfer, wait for completion, or request an advance document? A transfer that already arrived must remain recordable even if a UI workflow disallows initiating it.
4. **Boundary:** Which choices are consumer business policy and which are package-level public guarantees? A setting must not bypass ledger integrity or fiscal-document safeguards.
5. **Fiscal context:** What information must the consumer provide so the appropriate fiscal treatment can be determined? Do not promise that a generic configuration validates legal compliance.
6. **Compatibility:** What happens to existing consumers and persisted states? No defaults, migrations or version classification are decided here.

**Recorded consumer policy (clientes, 2026-10-31):** clientes proposes, as its own per-installation policy and without requesting any package setting, that a top-up proforma admits any amount received; that an ordinary proforma is whole-or-nothing until AID-1815 ships; and that a transfer that arrived anyway is recorded unallocated, with staff choosing its destination. The only package behaviour it relies on exists in 9.1 (an unallocated receipt that becomes a credit entry). This records one consumer's policy; the general question of configuration for other consumers stays open.
