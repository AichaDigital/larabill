# API surface taxonomy (`@api` / `@internal`)

> AID-413 (T-F of the consumer↔package boundary work, AID-407 spec §3). Every classlike under `src/` carries exactly one class-level tag, enforced in CI by `tests/Unit/Contract/SurfaceTaxonomyTest.php`. This document records the criteria so the classification stays conscious — it is the package-level input for the umbrella STD (T-A).

## The two tags

- **`@api`** — supported public surface. Consumers may depend on it; changes follow semver (added surface → at least minor; removed surface or changed signatures/semantics → major, with an UPGRADE guide in the dist). The contract snapshots (`tests/Contract/snapshots/`, AID-412) additionally freeze the full surface of the seven contract models.
- **`@internal`** — implementation detail. May change in any release without notice. Consumers depending on it do so at their own risk.

`@deprecated` composes with either tag: a deprecated public class keeps its `@api` until the announced removal major (precedent: `BillingService` — deprecated in v4.1.0/AID-390, kept `@api` through v5.x, removed in v6.0.0/AID-423; current wave: the ADR-015 shim layer — `ServiceStatus`, `BillingFrequency`, `CancellationType`, `ServiceCancelled`, and the frequency-signature methods on `Article`, `ArticleOverride`, `ArticleServiceStatus`, `ArticlePriceService`, `PricingService`, `ServiceLifecycleService` — deprecated in v7.0.0, kept `@api` through 7.x, removed in 8.0; the shim → replacement contract is the §5 mapping table of **UPGRADE-7.0.md**). Per STABILITY.md, a deprecation lives through at least one full major before removal.

## Guiding principle

**When in doubt → `@internal`.** Promoting `@internal` to `@api` later is a harmless minor; demoting `@api` is a breaking change. Real consumer usage (surveyed across the `clientes` app, 150 references, 2026-07-11) decided the borderline cases.

### Services split

- **`@api` (19):** `InvoiceService` (**ADR-016 PR-2:** the lifecycle methods `issue()`, `cancelPreparedInvoice()` and `convertAndIssue()` joined the surface and `convertProformaToInvoice()` now returns the typed `ProformaConversionResult` — its `createInvoice()` is two-phase for FISCAL types, proforma births verbatim §3.1; the service-signature surface is pinned by the new service contract snapshots, §12/V5), `InvoiceNumberingService` (**ADR-016 PR-2:** `generateNumber()` gained the optional trailing issuance-instant parameter; `generateNumberInTransaction()` is class-`@api` but method-`@internal` — the transaction-less path that assumes the ambient transaction, called only by `issue()`), `InvoiceSeriesResolver` (AID-307 — the single source of the fiscal series), `InvoiceVerifactuService`, `TaxCalculationService`, `FiscalChangeDetector` (real consumer usage), `RecurringBillingService` (AID-836: emits through `InvoiceService::createInvoice()` inside an atomic per-service boundary; its constructor's `$invoiceNumbering`/`$seriesResolver` params are deprecated-in-prose for removal in the next major, and it optionally receives a consumer-bound `RecurringEmissionHookContract`), `ServiceLifecycleService`, `GroupedPaymentService`, `PricingService`, `EuSalesThresholdService`, `DestinationVatService`, `CommissionCalculationService` (consumer-orchestrated domain), `ArticlePriceService` (AID-601/ADR-012 — the locked write path that guarantees the article price non-overlap invariant), `ArticleOverrideService` (AID-974 — the same for the CUSTOMER override invariant: at most one active override per customer/article pair on any given date, serialised by a lock on the parent `articles` row; create-only and always active by design), `SettlementService` (**ADR-016 PR-2:** `settleUnconsumed()` gained the additive optional settlement-operation key input and both keyed routes carry the §7.3 adoption-resume branch) and `SettlementArithmetic` (both AID-971/ADR-014 — the three corrective artifacts and the proportional unconsumed-time calculator; the calculator is pure and never invoked by the services themselves), `GuaranteeService` (ADR-015/AID-953 — the guarantee operations registry and the two-phase collision-and-adoption settlement: `openOperation()`, `settle()`, `confirmRefund()`, `guaranteeWindowEndsAt()` over the FROZEN `guarantee_days` snapshot) and `PlanChangeService` (ADR-015/AID-953 — the configurable plan-change flow: close with the ADR-014 abono + successor as a NEW contract, `restart` | `align_end`, eligibility enforced inside the atomic `FOR UPDATE` boundary). (`BillingService`, formerly `@api` + `@deprecated`, was removed in v6.0.0/AID-423.)
- **`@internal` (10):** `FiscalIntegrityChecker` (creating-hook mechanism; its public exception is the contract), `ModelMappingService` (the config keys are public, not the resolver), `CacheService`, `PDFService`/`DomPDFService`/`DefaultPDFConnector`/`FiscalContentValidator` (public surface is `Invoice::generatePDF()` + `PDFConnectorInterface` + the typed exceptions; the validator's contract is `FiscalContentMissingException` — ADR-011), `VerifactuAdapter`, `FakeFiscalVerification` (test double; promotable), `VatCalculationStrategy` (default implementation — the contract is the API).
